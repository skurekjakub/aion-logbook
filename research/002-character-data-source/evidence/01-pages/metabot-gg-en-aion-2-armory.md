## Where the data comes from

Profiles show the public character data the official AION 2 site publishes for every character: level, class, combat power, item level, equipped gear, stats, skills, Daevanion boards, titles, wings and pet. We keep a copy so pages load quickly and link each item and skill to our game database.

About the AION 2 Armory

The Armory reads the public character data the official AION 2 site publishes for 106 servers in 5 regions (NA East, NA West, Europe, Asia, and Latin America). Each profile shows the equipped gear and its enchant level, the six primary and ten deity stats, learned skills and stigmas, Daevanion board progress, titles, wings and pet, and links every item, skill, title, wing and mount to its page in our database. A profile refreshes when you open it if the stored copy is older than 30 minutes; 156,333 characters are in the Armory so far.

## AION 2 Armory FAQ

### How do I look up an AION 2 character?

Pick the region (NA East, NA West, Europe, Asia, and Latin America), type the character name and press Enter. The Armory searches the official character list and opens the profile with gear, stats, skills and Daevanion progress.

### How many AION 2 servers can I search?

106 servers in 5 regions: NA East 18, NA West 10, Europe 46, Asia 20, and Latin America 12. Each region has its own servers, so choose the region before you search.

### How often is character data updated?

When you open a profile whose stored copy is older than 30 minutes, it is refreshed from the official data. The Refresh button on a profile works once every 10 minutes per character.

### Why can't I find my character?

Check the region first: each region has its own servers (NA East, NA West, Europe, Asia, and Latin America). The official search matches part of a name, so try fewer letters if you are unsure of the exact spelling.

### Does MetaBot calculate combat power?

No. Combat power and item level are the numbers the official character data reports. The formula is not in the game files, so we never recompute or estimate them.

### Can I hide my character from the Armory?

Yes. Contact us through the methodology page with the region, server and character name, and we hide that profile.
