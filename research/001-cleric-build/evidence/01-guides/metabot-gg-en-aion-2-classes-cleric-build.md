Rotation

Drop the aura, apply torment, hit the follow-up every 3 seconds, thunder and the debuff mark between, basic attack for MP, and heal only when the bar actually drops.

Why this build

- The torment debuff and the follow-up that only hits tormented targets are maxed first: that pair is almost all of the Cleric's solo damage.
- The aura is free damage every 30 seconds; the single-target heal and the party heal-over-time are maxed so one cast covers a dungeon pull.
- Crit and crit-damage passives over heal-boost: while leveling you kill more than you heal. The debuff mark at Lv. 7 and the cleanse heal at Lv. 5 are there for bosses.
- Stigmas: the party damage-and-tolerance buff at 22 and the attacking aura at 27 are the two most used by top Clerics; the DoT nuke at 32 and the resurrection at 37 round out dungeons.

Rotation

Mark the focus target, torment it, follow up, drop the DoT nuke, thunder, then cleanse and heal whoever is lowest; the basic attack keeps your MP up between heals.

Why this build

- The debuff mark is maxed here: it cuts the enemy's damage and healing, which is worth more than your own damage in a group fight.
- All three heals maxed, including the cleanse heal: removing a stun or a fear from a teammate wins more fights than any damage skill.
- The defence passive replaces crit damage, heal-boost sits at Lv. 7, and the aura drops to Lv. 5: in PvP the aura is a distraction, not a damage source.
- Stigmas: the party buff, the cleanse-and-heal at 27, the full immunity at 32 and the DoT nuke at 37 for the rare kill window.

## How skill points work for the Cleric

Calculated

Budget and prices from the client's Exp and skill acquire tables.

Skill points start at level 4 and add up to 203 by level 45. Stigma points start at level 23 and reach 29 by level 45. Both are running totals: the table below shows what a Cleric has at each milestone.

Level 1 of every active skill and passive comes free when it unlocks (the client lists no cost for it). Each higher level costs skill points (Lv. 2–4: 1, Lv. 5–7: 2, and Lv. 8–10: 4), so one skill to Lv. 10 costs 21 points and all 22 skills cost 462. With 203 points at level 45 you can fully train about 9 of them, which is the core choice of a Cleric build.

The client prices skill levels only up to Lv. 10; the skills go to Lv. 40. Daevanion skill nodes add up to +4 per skill, and the planner's bonus field covers levels from other sources. Specialization slots open at skill levels 8, 12, and 20: 1 at Lv. 10, 2 with the full Daevanion bonus.

Stigma skills cost stigma points from level 1 (Lv. 1–5: 1, Lv. 6–10: 2, Lv. 11–15: 4, and Lv. 16–20: 8). One stigma to Lv. 20 costs 75. The 29 stigma points at level 45 train all 4 slotted stigmas to Lv. 6, or a single one to Lv. 13.

## Cleric build FAQ

### What is the best Cleric PvE build in AION 2?

Among 151 tracked top Cleric players, the most used active skills are Earth's Retribution, Judgment Thunder, Light of Regeneration, Condemnation, and Healing Light and the most used stigmas are Light of Protection, Noble Aura, Earth Punishment, and Summon Resurrection. The Endgame PvE card above shows every skill with its level and pick rate; load it into the planner to adjust it.

### What is the Cleric rotation?

Open with Light of Protection, then cycle Divine Aura, Noble Aura, Chain of Torment, Condemnation, Earth Punishment, Judgment Thunder, Healing Light, and Earth's Retribution, using the free basic attack whenever everything else is on cooldown. Drop the aura, apply torment, hit the follow-up every 3 seconds, thunder and the debuff mark between, basic attack for MP, and heal only when the bar actually drops.

### Which stigmas should a Cleric use first?

Slot them in this order as the slots open: Lv. 22: Light of Protection, Lv. 27: Noble Aura, Lv. 32: Earth Punishment, and Lv. 37: Summon Resurrection. Train Light of Protection first; it is the slot you hold the longest.

### What is a good combat power for a Cleric in AION 2?

Among the 1,560 top Cleric characters we track, the median combat power is 55,881 and the top 10% start at 69,974. Their average item level is 1,149.

### What stigmas do top Cleric players use?

Light of Protection is the most used stigma among 151 top Cleric players with a loadout read, equipped by 87%. The most used set: Light of Protection (87%), Noble Aura (79%), Earth Punishment (65%), and Summon Resurrection (64%).

### What weapon do top Cleric players use?

Spiritforged Mace is the most used main-hand weapon among 151 top Cleric players (43%), at an average enchant of +9.3.

### What is the best Cleric build in AION 2?

There is no single best Cleric build: at level 45 you have 203 skill points, enough to train about 9 of 22 skills to Lv. 10, plus 29 stigma points for 4 stigma slots. A build is the choice of which skills get those points, which of the 5 specializations each active skill uses and which of the 13 stigmas you slot. Use the planner above to compare options within the real budget.

### How many skill points does a Cleric get?

Skill points start at level 4 and total 203 by level 45. Level 1 of each skill is free; each level up to 10 costs skill points (Lv. 2–4: 1, Lv. 5–7: 2, and Lv. 8–10: 4), 21 per skill in all.
