3. 🌟 Cleric Stigma Recommendations by Content: Dungeon & PVP Guide

· Expeditions & Normal Dungeons:
· Essential Stigmas: Power of Life, Earth's Punishment, Noble Aura, Light of Possession.
· Additional Considerations: Prayer of Amplification (for DPS boost), Salvation (to counter the laser pattern in Hall of Illusions), Power of Uriel (to counter the erosion mechanic).
· If you have a Templar in your party, you can substitute with Immunity or Prayer of Amplification.
· For Care Purposes:
· Summoned Hatchling (for frequently dying party members), Absolution, Power of Life, Light of Possession, Earth's Punishment.
· Power of Uriel is still useful in PVE even though its PVP gap has been removed.
· If there's a Guardian in the party, consider Prayer of Amplification or Noble Aura instead of Power of Uriel.
· Hall of Illusions:
· Use the Salvation stigma to increase survivability during the grouped laser pattern.
· Sanctuary:
· Use the Salvation stigma to balance survival and healing.
· Also consider Power of Uriel to prepare for the final erosion mechanic.
· PVP (Party):
· Essential: Summoned Hatchling, Power of Uriel.
· Use in combination with Power of Life or Absolution.
· Light of Possession is also highly recommended.
· PVP (Solo):
· Recommended: Light of Possession, Power of Uriel, Noble Aura, Power of Life, Salvation.
· Earth's Punishment is effective for dealing damage to tanky opponents (Guardians, Gladiators, etc.).
· Key Summary:
· In PVE, Light of Possession, Power of Life, Noble Aura, and Earth's Punishment are core. Other stigmas can be flexibly swapped based on the situation.
· There's no single correct answer for Cleric stigmas; it's important to find the optimal combination for each dungeon and PVP scenario.

4. 📊 DPS Measurement Results & Arcana Setting Tips

· In the 1-minute DPS test against a training dummy, the damage contribution from Troops significantly increased. Using the Noble Aura stigma also greatly boosted damage.
· Upon reaching level 20 for Divine Retribution, the discharge damage increases. Sacred Energy contributed about 7% of total damage, Judgment Bolt 4%, and Divine Punishment 7%.
· Reaching level 20 for Arcana skills has a significant impact on DPS, so aim to level up useful skills to 20 as much as possible.
· For recommended Arcana card combinations, try using cards that include the skills Danjae, Kwegwang, Troops, and Response Skills to efficiently level them up without wasting any skills.
