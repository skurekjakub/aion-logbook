# AION 2 Patch Notes — January 28, 2026

The January 28, 2026 update adds a skill-macro feature and a per-character warehouse, applies a PvP damage-reduction pass, lowers the Stigma reset cost, and ships a broad class-balance pass. Translated from the official Korean update note.

Last updated: January 28, 2026

## Content & Systems

- Skill Macro added: chain basic light/heavy attacks for easier canceling. Add steps with per-step delays (one light or heavy attack each), bind to a hotkey in Settings, and hold to run; manual input always takes priority.
- Character Warehouse added: the warehouse now lets you choose Character or Server storage, each with its own quick-store settings (usable directly in crafting/conversion/enhancement); the old “Warehouse Expansion” item is renamed “Server Warehouse Expansion.”
- PvP balance: PvP damage is reduced further overall, while the item-level-gap correction is eased so you deal more damage to similarly-geared targets.
- Stigma point reset cost lowered to 10,000 Kinah per point; weekly accumulated-play reward thresholds for Exploration Odd Energy adjusted.
- Abyss: Middle Chaotic Ereshranta monster stats slightly lowered with a +40% Kinah/Abyss-point buff; Lower/Middle boss stats raised.
- Systems: Sanctuary entry no longer checks remaining reward count (you can play with challenges left, but get no Odd Energy Cube); gathering level changed to 45; an “appearance view / try-on” mode added to other characters' info.

## Class Changes

Common

- Survival Stance, Health Boost, Sixth Sense and Blessing of Life gain a max-HP stat; Shock Clear Spec 1 damage resistance 30% → 50%.
- Buff-dispel skills (Blade Toss, Infiltrate, Ensnaring Trap, Voice of Doom) now break all shields; shock-resistance scrolls become status-resistance scrolls with freeze/fear/deadwood/sleep/seal defense; dispel can now remove invincibility.

## Promotion, QoL & fixes

- Promotion: a “Character Warehouse Expansion” item in the Chengarung shop (200 Quna); the membership “remote warehouse” benefit now covers both server and character warehouses.
- QoL: a minimap clock (game/local time), F-key support on more UI buttons, inheritance-UI improvements, ranking pages showing class rankings, and a longer dungeon reconnect grace (1 → 10 min).
- Fixes: numerous Dying Dramata's Nest / Shattered Arkanis / Nightmare fixes, gear soul-bound to other characters no longer usable as materials, and the Cradle of Nihility / Sunken Temple of Life openings deferred to Feb 11 / Feb 25.
- Reclaim: correction of the January 21 Season 1 reward-payout error (incorrect rewards reclaimed except Oath Coins; correct rewards re-issued).
