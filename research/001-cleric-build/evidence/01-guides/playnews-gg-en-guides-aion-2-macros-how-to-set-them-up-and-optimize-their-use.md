Aion 2 Macros: How to Set Them Up and Optimize Their Use

Alexandre Jollier

October 6, 2026 at 5:50 PM

Follow us on

## In brief

Aion 2 macros allow you to chain up to 4 skills with a single key, drastically reducing your APM in combat. This guide shows you how to configure them properly, optimize them based on skill priority, and use them to maximize your DPS without unnecessary strain.

In Aion 2, macros let you chain multiple skills with a single keypress. A well-configured macro reduces your APM (actions per minute) and makes the game noticeably more comfortable to play. Here's how to create and use your first macro.

## Step 1: Assign a macro key

The macro key is not set by default: without it, you can't use your macros, even if they're configured.

- Press Esc and open Settings.

- Go to Key Settings, General tab.

- Scroll down to the Gameplay section and find the Macro option (the very first one).

- Assign the key of your choice.

Right-click, mouse 3, or mouse 4 are the most common choices in MMOs, but pick whatever suits you: you'll be holding it down for a good chunk of combat, so choose a comfortable key.

## Step 2: Understand skill order and priority

You can assign up to 4 skills to the same button on the hotbar. It works simply: with each press, your character tries to use the lowest skill in the stack, in a loop; if it can't be used, it moves up to the one above, and so on.

The idea: place skills with a cooldown or activation conditions at the bottom, and skills with instant cast, no cooldown above. The character spams the latter, but as soon as the skills below become available and meet their conditions, they take priority.

Without a macro, you have to mash the key to chain the 4 skills. With a macro, a single key forces the game to press that button in the background - and by assigning multiple slots to the macro, you multiply the effect.

## Step 3: Set up the macro

- Open the Skills menu (K).

- Click the Macro button in the top right to open the configuration screen.

- Click Add Macro then select the skill icon: the hotbar will appear.

- Choose a button on the bar and save it: all skills on that button are added to the macro.

- To add more, click Add Macro again and repeat.

Important

The macro references the button on the bar, not the specific skills it contains. If you change the skills in that slot, the macro adapts automatically.

## Which skills to put in a macro

There are roughly six types of skills (varying by class): basic MP recovery, no-cooldown spam, spam with a 5-20s cooldown, situational skills, ultimate skills (45-60s), and personal buffs.

Avoid putting in MP recovery (your safety net when MP runs low), personal buffs, and ultimates (to be used strategically). Prioritize spam skills: those with 0 cooldown at the top of the stack, those with short cooldowns below. Situational skills should only be added if relevant (e.g. a skill that only activates on a stunned enemy). Finally, only add skills that are sufficiently upgraded: a level 0 skill would only slow down your DPS.

## How to use a macro

Test it in combat or on a training dummy: simply hold down the macro key and the character will execute the skills in order. In combat, also keep your basic skill key held down at all times: this allows for animation canceling (more DPS) and keeps your MP at the cap during your combos.

## Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my macro work even after configuring it?

You probably didn't assign a macro key in Settings (Esc > Settings > Key Settings > Gameplay > Macro). Without this step, no macro is executable, no matter how it's configured. Choose a comfortable key like right-click or mouse 4.

How many skills can I add to a single macro?

You can assign up to 4 skills to the same button on your hotbar. A macro looks at the button, not the exact skills it contains: if you change the skills in that slot, the macro adapts automatically.

Do I really need to put basic skills at the top and ultimates at the bottom?

Yes, absolutely. Skills with no cooldown should be at the top for constant spamming, while those with cooldown/conditions go at the bottom so the system tests them in priority first. Ultimates and buffs shouldn't be in a macro: keep them for manual and strategic use.

What's the real benefit of a macro if I still have to hold the key down?

Huge benefit: instead of mashing multiple keys to chain 4 skills, you only hold one. This reduces your APM, decreases strain, and makes the game much more comfortable, especially during long grinding or intensive farming.

Should I add my character's basic skill to the macro?

No, keep it separate. In combat, you must hold your basic skill key permanently: this allows animation cancelling (higher DPS) and keeps your mana points at the cap during your combos. The macro handles specialized skills.

## Key takeaways

- First assign a macro key in Settings (Gameplay > Macro): without it, your macros won't work, even if configured.

- The macro works in a loop: it tests the lowest skill first, then moves up to higher ones if that skill isn't available.

- Place your skills with no cooldown at the top of the stack and those with cooldown/conditions at the bottom so the priority system automatically switches between them.

- Avoid mana regeneration, personal buffs, and ultimates in macros: reserve them for manual play for more strategic control.

- Always test your macro on the training dummy while holding the key and keep your basic skill pressed permanently for animation cancelling.
