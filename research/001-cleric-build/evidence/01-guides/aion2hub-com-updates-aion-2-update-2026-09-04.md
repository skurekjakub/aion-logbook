# AION 2 Patch Notes — September 4, 2026 (Emergency Maintenance: Class Care)

An off-cycle 'class care' balance pass, pulled forward into a 90-minute emergency maintenance so that it lands separately from the new Sanctuary due on September 9. The Templar takes the largest share: the two attack chains swap which cooldown their spec #4 reduces, Warding Strike is lengthened to 30s to pay for it, the Executor buff doubles to 20s, and Judgment trades 5% PvE damage for 7% less in PvP. The Assassin's Illusive Clone drops to a 90s cooldown, Elemental Fusion and the Cleric's Bolt each gain 20% PvE damage, Prayer of Amplification is rebuilt around damage amplification instead of cooldown, and Judgment, Ambush Stance and Dark Crush are all fixed for the invulnerability state. Two days after launch the Spacetime Rift Domination reopens its headcount from 60–200 to 25–500, and Watcher Kaira's spawn base shifts to 01:00. Translated from the official Korean notice.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

## Maintenance window & why it happened

The second unscheduled maintenance in as many days, and unlike the September 3 one this is a real balance patch. The notice went up on the morning of September 3 as an advance warning with the contents listed as 'to be announced', and the contents were appended to the same article at 03:00 on September 4, ninety minutes before the servers went down.

| Date and time | September 4, 2026 (Fri), 04:30 – 06:00 KST (1 hour 30 minutes) |
| Impact | The game cannot be played for the duration. |
| Reason given | To apply the class balance adjustments ahead of the new Sanctuary 'Snowfield of Sorrow' due on September 9 (Wed), so that a balance pass and a content patch do not land at the same time. |

In the operator's own words the aim is to reduce the confusion that would come from applying the new Sanctuary and the class balance adjustments simultaneously, and to provide a more stable game environment. The note apologises for the run of back-to-back emergency maintenances.

## Class Care

'Class care' is the operator's term for an off-cycle balance pass. This one delivers the four changes previewed at the end of the September 2 note — the Templar's Shield Smite, the Assassin's Illusive Clone, the Spiritmaster's post-bugfix DPS and the Cleric's PvE damage — and goes considerably further, adding a Ranger and Chanter pass and a set of fixes for skills that misbehaved while the character was invulnerable.

The Templar gets the largest share. The two attack chains swap which cooldown they reduce: the Vicious Strike chain's spec #4 now shortens Warding Strike instead of Punishment, while the Pummel chain's spec #4 now shortens Punishment instead of Warding Strike. Warding Strike's own cooldown is lengthened to pay for it, and the Executor buff doubles in duration.

## Spacetime Rift Domination fixes

Two days after the Domination launched in the September 2 patch, its headcount limits are thrown wide open in both directions — the floor drops so matches can actually fill, and the ceiling more than doubles.

|  | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participants per side | Minimum 60 – maximum 200 | Minimum 25 – maximum 500 |

- Fixed the objective Monoliths not disappearing and remaining in place after the Spacetime Rift Domination ended.

## Abyss fixes

- The spawn start time of 'Watcher Kaira', who appears in Lower Reshanta in the Abyss, is changed. The boss now spawns every 4 hours counted from 01:00, rather than from 00:00 as announced on September 2.

## Frequently asked questions

- What is 'class care' in AION 2?Class care is the operator's own term for an off-cycle class balance pass, applied outside the regular Wednesday maintenance. This one ran in a 90-minute emergency maintenance on September 4, 2026, and the stated reason for pulling it forward was to keep the balance adjustments separate from the new Sanctuary content patch due on September 9, so the two would not land together and cause confusion.
- What changed for the Templar in the AION 2 September 4 class care patch?The two attack chains swap which cooldown they shorten. The Vicious Strike, Decisive Strike, Desperate Strike and Threatening Blow chain's spec #4 now cuts 2s from Warding Strike instead of Punishment, and gains 20% PvE damage. The Pummel and Punishing Strike chain's spec #4 now cuts 1s from Punishment instead of Warding Strike, and loses 7% PvP damage. Warding Strike's own cooldown is lengthened from 25s to 30s, Punishment's Executor buff doubles from 10s to 20s while the skill loses 10% PvE damage, and Judgment gains 5% PvE damage but loses 7% in PvP. Shield Smite also becomes a move-enabled skill with an improved motion.
- How many players can join the AION 2 Spacetime Rift Domination now?From the September 4, 2026 maintenance the limits are a minimum of 25 and a maximum of 500 players per side. That replaces the minimum of 60 and maximum of 200 announced when the Domination launched on September 2, lowering the floor so matches can fill and more than doubling the ceiling. The same patch also fixed the objective Monoliths remaining in place instead of disappearing once a Domination ended.
- What was fixed about the invulnerability state in AION 2?Three separate skills were not working correctly while the character was in an invulnerability state, and all three were fixed in this patch: the Templar's Judgment was not activating, the Assassin's Ambush Stance passive was not working, and the Chanter's Dark Crush was not activating.
- When does Watcher Kaira spawn in AION 2 after September 4?Watcher Kaira spawns in Lower Reshanta every four hours counted from 01:00. The September 2 patch had made the spawn guaranteed rather than a 20% chance and set the cycle from 00:00; this patch shifts only the base time by an hour, leaving the four-hour cycle and the random spawn point unchanged.
