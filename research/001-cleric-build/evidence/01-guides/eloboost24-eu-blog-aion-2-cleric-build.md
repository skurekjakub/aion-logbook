###### AION 2 Cleric Build Guide (Global 2026)

The best AION 2 Cleric build levels Healing Light and Radiant Recovery first, keeps Chain of Torment on the target between heals, registers Light of Protection, Absolution, Yustiel's Power and Summon Resurrection as Stigmas at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37, and uses a damage Theostone in PvE. The Cleric is the only pure Healer in the global client, but its offensive kit is better than it looks.

This guide is built for the global version that launched on October 5, 2026, using the skill data in the global launch client. There is no global ranking data yet, so these picks come from what the Cleric's kit rewards on paper, with the reasoning shown, not from leaderboards.

## How the Cleric Plays in AION 2

The Cleric fights with a mace and is the only pure Healer in the global client. Groups need you, which means finding a party is easy and your mistakes are very visible.

The part most new Clerics miss is the damage side. Chain of Torment lowers the target's PvE Damage Tolerance for the whole party. A Cleric who heals with short cooldowns and keeps that debuff up between heals is adding real damage to the group, not just keeping it alive.

## How Cleric Builds Work on Global

Every class on global has 12 active skills, 10 passives and 13 Stigma skills. Skill points take an active or passive from level 1 to 10 for 21 points, Daevanion boards add up to four more levels, and you register four Stigmas at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37. If you want the full picture of why some Korean guides show different numbers, read our explanation of why Korean AION 2 builds do not work on global.

## Best Cleric Skills to Level First

Healing Light is your workhorse: a 6 second cooldown heal that automatically targets the party member with the lowest HP. Radiant Recovery heals the party on a 12 second cooldown and removes a debuff. Light of Regeneration is a 30 second party heal over time.

Chain of Torment reduces the enemy's PvE Damage Tolerance by 5%. With 21 points needed per skill, prioritise Healing Light and Radiant Recovery.

## Best Cleric Passives

Healing Enhancement raises your Heal Boost, and Warm Benediction adds +6% Max HP. At level 21, Earth's Grace gives +10.5% Critical Damage Boost, and at 25, Radiant Benediction heals the party when you attack, which rewards you for fighting between heals.

## Cleric Specializations

On Healing Light, take +2 consecutive uses first, then -2 seconds cooldown at skill level 16. On Radiant Recovery, take the option that removes up to two debuffs. On Chain of Torment, the level 12 option adds a further +10% PvE Damage Tolerance reduction, which is a big party damage increase.

## Cleric Daevanion Path

Walk Nezekan's Healing Enhancement and Warm Benediction pairs first, then Zikel's Radiant Benediction.

## Best Cleric Weapon and Theostone

Use an Epic or Unique mace. In PvE, any damage Theostone works, because you attack less than a damage class. In PvP, a Stun or Seal Theostone helps you peel attackers off yourself.

## Cleric PvP Notes

In PvP, the Cleric is the first target in every fight. Keep a Stun or Seal Theostone, save Radiant Recovery for debuffs that matter, and stay behind your frontline.

## Common Cleric Mistakes

-

Only healing and never applying Chain of Torment.

-

Running Light of Protection while leveling solo, when Earth Punishment is better.

-

Doubling up debuffs with a Chanter. Chain of Torment does not stack with the Chanter's Earth's Promise.

-

Copying a Korean healer build that assumes more Stigma slots than global has.

## Do Not Copy a Korean Cleric Build Blindly

Korea and Taiwan have been playing AION 2 since November 2025, so most Cleric guides online were written for their servers. Those servers are now on a different version: a level 50 cap instead of 45, Stigma levels up to 25 instead of 20, extra content, and a ninth class. A Korean Cleric build can recommend Stigmas, skill levels or gear you cannot reach on global yet. Use those guides for class logic, not for numbers. Our Korean vs global builds breakdown covers every difference.

## FAQ

What is the best Cleric build in AION 2? Level Healing Light and Radiant Recovery first, keep Chain of Torment on targets, register Light of Protection, Absolution, Yustiel's Power and Summon Resurrection as Stigmas, and use a damage Theostone in PvE.

Is the Cleric the only healer in AION 2? It is the only pure Healer. The Chanter is a hybrid tagged as both Healer and Dealer.

Does the Cleric's debuff stack with the Chanter's? No. Chain of Torment and the Chanter's Earth's Promise do not apply together, and Light of Protection does not stack with Undefeated Mantra.

What Stigma should a Cleric take while leveling? Earth Punishment, then swap to Light of Protection for dungeons.

Do these Cleric builds work on Korean servers? The class logic does, but the numbers are set for the global launch client: level 45, four Stigma slots and Stigma level 20. Korean servers are on a later version.
