# AION 2 Cleric Build: Best Healer Skills, Stigmas and Gear (Dungeons & PvP)

Home›Guides›AION 2 Cleric Build: Best Healer Skills, Stigmas and Gear (Dungeons & PvP)

Published Oct 1, 2026Updated Oct 2, 2026Read Time 15 min

The Cleric is built around keeping your party alive, but there is more to the class than simply watching health bars.

Quick Answer

Cleric is AION 2's dedicated healer, but it can also deal solid damage and provide strong party support. Its healing, DPS and support abilities make it useful across both PvE and PvP.

Full guide below ↓

The Cleric is AION 2‘s main healer, built around keeping the party alive while still having enough damage and utility to contribute between healing windows. Your skill choices, Stigmas and gear all affect how well you can handle different content.

The build you use for a dungeon does not always need to be the same one you take into PvP. Dungeons place more emphasis on sustained healing, party utility and encounter mechanics, while PvP puts more pressure on survival, cleansing and defensive tools. In this guide, I will cover the skills, Stigmas and gear priorities that matter most for both.

## Best PvP & Dungeon Cleric Build for AION 2

Let’s first go over the stats you should prioritize based on the type of Cleric build you are playing.

### Healer Cleric Stats

If you are building your Cleric primarily for healing, prioritize Wisdom [Lumiel] and Life [Yustiel] as your main God Stats, along with Constitution and Power as your Primary Stats. These stats give you a strong foundation for a dedicated support build.

You should also look for plenty of HP and Defense so you can survive longer when enemies start targeting you. A dead healer cannot keep the party alive, so having enough durability is important in both dungeons and PvP.

Combat Speed is another useful stat for Clerics because it helps you cast your skills more quickly. Move Speed also helps you reposition around the battlefield, which becomes particularly useful when you need to avoid mechanics or move back into healing range.

For PvP, I would put additional focus on Status Effect Resist. Enemy players can use crowd control to interrupt your positioning and prevent you from supporting your team, so resisting those effects can make a big difference.

For PvE, Heal Boost becomes more important because your main job is keeping the party alive through sustained damage.

### DPS/Hybrid Cleric Stats

If you plan to use your Cleric as a DPS or hybrid build, your priorities change. In this case, focus on Precision and Intelligence as your main Primary Stats, along with Destruction [Zikel] and Death [Triniel] as your main God Stats.

For your other stats, Attack, Critical Hit and Combat Speed are useful priorities. Attack is particularly valuable because Cleric’s healing also scales from it, giving a hybrid build some extra value when switching between damage and support.

This type of build gives you more offensive pressure when your party does not need constant healing. You can deal damage during safer parts of an encounter, then switch back to your healing abilities when the group starts taking heavy damage.

The exact balance depends on how much healing your group needs. If you are playing as the main healer, keep your survivability and healing stats as the priority. If you are deliberately building around damage, you can invest more heavily into your offensive stats once your core survivability is covered.

### Best Cleric Active Skills

The Cleric has a mix of healing, offensive, debuff and utility skills, so you should not treat every ability the same. Your skill priorities depend on whether you are playing as a dedicated healer or building around a hybrid setup.

For a healing Cleric, your main priority should be getting your core healing skills developed first. Once your healing is reliable, you can put more points into the offensive and debuff skills that let you contribute damage between healing windows.

#### Healing Skills

Healing Light should be one of your first major investments for a dedicated healer. It restores HP to you and the party member with the lowest HP within range, making it particularly useful when one player suddenly takes heavy damage. Its short cooldown also makes it a reliable emergency heal.

Getting Healing Light to Level 8 is a good early breakpoint because you unlock its first specialty choices. You can increase its consecutive uses, improve the healing when the target is below 50% HP or add a chance to remove a debuff. For a dedicated healer, the extra healing against low HP targets is particularly useful when your group regularly takes large bursts of damage.

At Level 12, Healing Light gains 2% additional HP restored. Level 16 then reduces its cooldown by 2 seconds, making deeper investment worthwhile once your other core skills are established.

Radiant Recovery is your other major party healing tool. It restores HP to you and nearby party members while also removing debuffs. That gives it value beyond simply recovering health, especially in encounters where the group is taking damage and dealing with status effects at the same time.

Take Radiant Recovery to Level 8 early and choose the specialty that matches the content you are playing. You can remove up to two debuffs, gain an additional consecutive cast or reduce the cooldown. If you regularly deal with multiple debuffs, the stronger cleanse has obvious value. If healing pressure is the bigger problem, the extra cast or cooldown reduction can be more useful.

At Level 12, Radiant Recovery provides 10% Incoming Heal for 10 seconds. Level 16 increases the amount of HP restored by 5%, making deeper investment worthwhile for a dedicated healer.

Light of Regeneration fills a different role because it provides healing over time. It restores HP to you and nearby party members every two seconds for 30 seconds, giving you sustained recovery during encounters with repeated party-wide damage.

At Level 8, you can choose between three effects: 5% more Defense while healing, 20% more healing when the target is at 50% HP or lower or 5% more Incoming Heal. At Level 12, you can gain 5% Move Speed while healing. Level 16 adds 5% PvE Damage Tolerance and 2.5% PvP Damage Tolerance while healing, giving the skill defensive value beyond its raw healing.

#### Offensive Skills

A Cleric should not spend the entire fight healing. Once the party is healthy, your offensive skills let you contribute damage and keep pressure on bosses.

Condemnation is one of the most important offensive skills because it forms the core of the Cleric’s damage cycle. It can only target enemies affected by Chain of Torment, so the two skills need to be used together. It also has a very short cooldown, allowing it to become a major part of your damage rotation.

The important breakpoint comes at Level 12, where a critical hit with Condemnation can reset its cooldown. This creates a much stronger damage cycle and is one of the main reasons to invest heavily into the skill.

Earth’s Retribution is another important part of the Cleric’s rotation. It deals damage and restores MP, giving you a reliable way to maintain your resources during longer fights.

At Level 8, you can improve the chance of triggering its Discharge chain. The more important breakpoint comes at Level 12, where landing Discharge reduces Bolt’s cooldown by 7 seconds. This gives Earth’s Retribution an important role in the Cleric’s offensive rotation beyond its MP recovery.

Bolt is your stronger charged attack. It can be charged through three levels, deals significantly more damage at higher charge levels and also deals Stagger Gauge damage.

Level 8 gives you several useful options, including 30% Skill Speed. You do not need to push Bolt ahead of your main Condemnation and Earth’s Retribution investments, but it becomes more valuable once those skills are developed enough to support its rotation.

Divine Aura summons a stationary aura that periodically attacks enemies near it. This gives the Cleric another source of damage that continues working after the skill has been placed.

At Level 8, you can increase its attack speed by 50% or choose other utility options. Level 12 extends its duration by three seconds, and Level 16 can reduce its cooldown by ten seconds. These later breakpoints make Divine Aura particularly useful for a damage focused or hybrid Cleric.

Judgment Thunder deals area damage around your selected target and can hit up to four enemies. It is particularly useful against groups of enemies and has a more specialized role in a Cleric’s damage setup.

This is not a skill I would rush ahead of your main damage tools. Level 16 is the important target because its specialty can activate Divine Punishment one additional time. For a dedicated healer, you can leave it lower until your core healing and boss damage skills are developed.

Lightning Strike Scattershot is more situational. It targets enemies that are already Staggered, so its usefulness depends on whether you can consistently create those stagger windows.

For most Clerics, there is no reason to prioritize this early. You can leave it at a low level and invest in it later if your build or the encounter gives you a reason to make greater use of stagger. Its later specialties can add Multi-Hit and reduce all skill cooldowns when it hits.

#### Debuff Skills

Chain of Torment is one of the most important skills in the Cleric’s damage setup. It deals damage over time and reduces the target’s PvE Damage Tolerance, allowing the entire party to deal more damage. Condemnation also requires the target to be affected by Chain of Torment.

At Level 8, you can extend the damage over time by three seconds, slow the target for five seconds or gain a chance to Knockdown the target. Level 12 adds 10% PvE Damage Tolerance reduction and 5% PvP Damage Tolerance reduction to the skill’s existing debuff.

Because Condemnation depends on it, treat these two skills as one damage package. Apply Chain of Torment, use Condemnation during its duration and keep the debuff active so you can continue the cycle.

Debilitating Mark provides another debuff option. Its base effect reduces the target’s PvE Damage Boost by 5% and also applies damage over time. One of its Level 8 specialties adds a 15% Defense reduction for the duration of the damage over time effect. At Level 12, another specialty adds 10% PvE Damage Boost reduction and 5% PvP Damage Boost reduction to its existing effect.

Debilitating Mark does not need to compete with your main healing skills for early investment. Level 8 is enough for its primary Defense reduction, with deeper investment becoming more useful when you want its additional effects.

#### Utility & Defensive Skills

Defiance is your main personal crowd control escape. It removes effects including Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost and Fear, then gives you Tenacity and temporary status effect immunity.

This becomes particularly valuable in PvP because losing control of your Cleric can leave your entire group without healing. It is also useful in difficult PvE encounters with dangerous crowd control.

At Level 8, you can choose effects such as restoring 10% HP when Defiance is used. Level 12 extends the Tenacity duration by 2 seconds, making that breakpoint useful for a defensive or PvP focused build. At Level 16, the Tenacity effect can also provide 50% PvE Damage Tolerance and 25% PvP Damage Tolerance.

### Recommended Active Skills Priority

Instead of trying to push every skill through the same levels, I would build the Cleric in stages. Start with your core skills:

1. Condemnation to Level 8
2. Earth’s Retribution to Level 8
3. Bolt to Level 8
4. Divine Aura to Level 8
5. Healing Light to Level 8
6. Radiant Recovery to Level 8
7. Chain of Torment to Level 8
8. Light of Regeneration to Level 8

This gives you the main damage cycle, healing tools and core utility.

For a healing focused Cleric, the next long-term investments should generally be Healing Light, Radiant Recovery and Light of Regeneration. Healing Light and Radiant Recovery gain important improvements at Level 12 and 16, while Light of Regeneration gains its stronger defensive effect at Level 16.

For a damage or hybrid Cleric, prioritize Condemnation and Earth’s Retribution toward Level 12. Chain of Torment is also worth pushing to Level 12 because of its stronger PvE Damage Tolerance reduction. Bolt and Divine Aura can then receive deeper investment as your offensive build develops.

Debilitating Mark can then reach Level 8 for its Defense reduction. Keep Judgment Thunder and Lightning Strike Scattershot lower until you have the points to invest in their more specialized effects. Defiance can also stay low until you need its crowd control utility.

Keep Judgment Thunder and Lightning Strike Scattershot lower on the priority list unless your content or build gives you a specific reason to invest in them. They have useful roles, but they should not take points away from the skills that form the core of your healing or damage rotation.

### Best Cleric Passive Skills

Cleric has 10 passive skills, and they cover healing, survivability, offensive scaling and PvP utility. You do not need to invest in every passive equally. Your priorities should change depending on whether you are mainly healing in dungeons, dealing damage or playing PvP.

For a general Cleric build, I would start with Warm Benediction, Immortal Veil, Empyrean Lord’s Grace and Earth’s Grace. These give you a useful mix of survivability and offensive stats that remain valuable across different types of content.

Warm Benediction increases your Max HP by 6% and Max MP by 7. This gives you a stronger health pool while also providing additional MP, making it a useful passive to invest in early.

Immortal Veil increases your Defense by 2% and Critical Hit Resist by 100. This is particularly useful when you want more survivability.

Empyrean Lord’s Grace is one of your main offensive passives. It gives you 100 Critical Hit, 0.2% Double Chance and adds 71 extra damage when you land an attack, with the additional damage having a 1 second cooldown. This makes it especially valuable for Clerics who spend more time dealing damage between their healing and support skills.

Earth’s Grace increases your Critical Damage Boost by 10.5 and gives you 100 Accuracy. This makes it another important offensive passive, particularly for damage focused or hybrid Cleric builds.

#### Passive for Dungeons

For dungeon content, Healing Enhancement becomes much more important if you are playing primarily as a healer. It increases your Heal Boost based on your Attack, so improving this passive directly supports your healing output.

#### PvP Passives

For PvP, Empyrean Lords’ Benediction becomes more useful because it increases your Block by 200 and restores 53 to 64 HP whenever you successfully block, with a 3 second cooldown. This gives you additional survivability when you are taking direct attacks.

Prayer of Concentration is another useful defensive option. When your HP reaches 50% or lower, it creates a shield that blocks 424 damage and increases your Heal Boost by 20% for 30 seconds. It has a 60 second cooldown.

For PvP, I would also keep Survival Willpower in mind. It increases Status Effect Resist by 17% and provides additional PvE and PvP Damage Tolerance after certain crowd control effects. This makes it another defensive option when surviving crowd control is a major concern.

### Cleric Stigmas

Stigmas give the Cleric another way to shape the build around the content you are playing. You can use them to add healing, party buffs, defensive effects or extra damage, so your setup does not need to stay the same for every activity.

For your first stigma investments, I would focus on Benevolence, Light of Protection and Yustiel’s Power if you are building around supporting your party. These give you a useful foundation for surviving difficult content and keeping the group supported.

Benevolence gives you a sustained healing effect that restores HP to you and nearby party members over time. It is useful for keeping the group topped up between heavier bursts of damage and becomes more valuable as you invest further into it.

Light of Protection is a party wide buff that improves offensive and defensive stats for you and nearby party members. This makes it useful even when you are not actively healing because the entire group benefits from the effect.

Yustiel’s Power is another strong support option. It provides temporary damage resistance to you and nearby party members, giving the group additional protection during dangerous parts of an encounter.

#### Healing Build

If you are playing as the group’s main healer, Absolution is one of the most useful stigmas to consider. It removes debuffs from you and nearby party members while also restoring HP, giving you both cleansing and healing in the same skill.

This makes Absolution particularly useful in dungeons where the party is regularly dealing with harmful status effects.

Benevolence also fits naturally into a healing setup because its healing over time gives the party sustained recovery between larger healing skills. At higher levels, it gains additional effects that further improve its usefulness during longer encounters.

You can also consider Salvation when an encounter has dangerous mechanics that require a temporary defensive window. It makes the caster immune to damage and status effects for a short period, although certain powerful attacks cannot be blocked by it.

Voice of Doom is another option when you need more than pure healing. It deals Earth damage to enemies around the selected target and applies additional damage over time. It also reduces enemy healing, making it more useful when you need offensive pressure or anti healing.

#### DPS Build

For a damage focused Cleric, the stigma setup changes considerably. Prayer of Amplification is one of the simplest offensive choices because it temporarily increases your Attack, giving your damage skills more power during its duration.

Noble Aura is another important DPS stigma. It summons an aura that follows you and attacks your target, adding another source of damage to your rotation. Its upgrades can further improve its damage and interactions with your offensive setup.

Earth Punishment is particularly valuable for a damage build because it works directly with Condemnation. Its effect can guarantee a critical hit from Condemnation when the required conditions are met, which works especially well with Condemnation’s critical hit cooldown reset.

Power Burst is more specialized. Its main value is the large amount of Stagger Gauge damage it provides, so it becomes useful when your group needs additional stagger damage during encounters with important stagger checks, including some dungeon bosses.

### Cleric Gear

Cleric gear is relatively straightforward because Attack also improves your healing. This means you can build around offensive stats without needing a completely separate gear setup for healing.

For your weapon and guard, look for Might, Precision, Combat Speed, Weapon Damage Boost and Damage Boost. These are all useful gear lines for Cleric.

For armor, the useful lines vary by slot. Attack Increase is useful on the helmet, Critical Damage Boost is valuable on the shoulders and Damage Boost is important on the chest. Combat Speed is a useful line for gloves, while the remaining pieces can provide Attack, movement speed, defensive stats and other useful lines.

Rings are particularly useful for Cleric because crafted rings can provide additional active skill levels. For a PvE build, skills such as Condemnation, Radiant Recovery, Earth’s Retribution and Bolt are useful targets.

You should also look for Cooldown Reduction as you progress. Cleric benefits from having important skills available more often, particularly Earth Punishment and Chain of Torment.

## That’s It

Building a good Cleric comes down to finding the right balance between healing, damage and support. Once you understand your skills, Stigmas and gear, you can adjust your build around the content you are playing instead of trying to use the same setup everywhere. With the right setup, Cleric can keep the party alive, contribute solid damage and handle a wide range of encounters.
