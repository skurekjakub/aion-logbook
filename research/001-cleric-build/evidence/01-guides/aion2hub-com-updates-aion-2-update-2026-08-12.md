# AION 2 Patch Notes — August 12, 2026

The August 12, 2026 update opens the Consumed Deus Research Base expedition and stages 1–4 of the Noiran's Hidden Legacy transcendence, which drops the new Dice and Lantern Arcana parts. The class section is a game-wide healing rework: Cleric and Chanter heals gain a 40m radius but lose 20–50% of their raw output, and both healing passives now scale with attack power instead of granting flat amplification. Alongside it come race-integrated Arena and Battlefield matching with fixed 1- or 5-player groups, the next Draconic and Abyss accessory tiers, a mobile HUD editor, a rebuilt Shop with the Closet Shop folded into it, and the end of the fromis_9 collaboration. Translated from the official Korean update note.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

## Content & Systems

August 12 is a full weekly patch. Two new instances open, the Draconic and Abyss accessory tiers get their next step, and the Arena/Battlefield matcher is rebuilt around race-integrated teams and fixed 1-player or 5-player group sizes.

Exploration and Conquest (Normal / Hard) open for the new Expedition 'Consumed Deus Research Base'.

### Arena / Battlefield

- Arena of Cooperation and Battlefield matching now builds teams regardless of race.
- The Battlefield can only be matched into as a solo or as a group of 5. Groups of 5 are matched only against other groups of 5; solo players are matched only against other solo players, and a class-balance correction is applied between the two teams.
- Players who take no part in combat for a set length of time are forcibly removed from the Battlefield.

### New equipment

New Draconic crafted gear

- Crafting blueprints for the 'Genesis Dragon Lord / Nemesis Dragon Lord' accessories are added.
- 'Genesis Dragon Lord / Nemesis Dragon Lord' accessories can be crafted using 'Splendent Noble Dragon Lord / Splendent Horned Dragon Lord' accessories as inheritance material.
- The crafting material 'Rampaging Fearsome Aura: Accessory (Soul-bound)' can be obtained by material-converting a 'Lava Heart' accessory, or the gear-change ticket obtainable from the new Expedition's guaranteed rewards.

New Abyss gear

- 'Guardian / Archon Praetorian Captain' accessories are added to the Abyss shop.
- A blueprint is added to inherit 'Guardian / Archon High Commander' accessories into 'Guardian / Archon Praetorian Captain' accessories.

### Mobile HUD UI editing

- A feature to directly edit part of the mobile HUD is added.
- While editing the HUD you can set the grid spacing, the UI scale, and whether snapping is used.
- UI size and position can be adjusted in detail, and elements can be hidden or unhidden.
- The Stigma slots and the Transformation slots are separated so each can be repositioned or resized independently.
- A feature is added to expand the consumable slots so all 8 can be seen at once.
- Clicking the [i] button in the HUD edit settings window opens a detailed control guide.

### Crafting rates

- Combo probability by mastery-level difference is added to the crafting probability information.
- Selecting the toggle button to the right of the base rate shows the combo probability information.

### Genus Insight

- An automatic analysis feature is added to Genus Insight.
- In the auto-analysis UI you can set the selectable slots, options and minimum grade, based on your current Genus Insight level.
- Auto-analysis ends as soon as one or more results match the slot, option and grade conditions you set.
- Auto-analysis stops if your Genus Insight level rises or if you run short of the required currency.

### Shugo Festival

- On the 'Mysterious Track', the effect triggered by colliding with a whirlwind changes from knockback to slow.

### Abyss

- Artifact occupation and the Abyss corridor are reset for the server rematch. The full server-matching table is published on the CM Hideout rather than in the note.

### Premium distribution: dice

- Dice can only be rolled from within the distribution range; if nobody wins the roll, the item is given at random to a party or force member in the same map.
- The Sanctuary continues to be exempt from the range restriction.

## Class Changes

The headline of this patch is a game-wide healing rework. Every major heal on the Cleric and Chanter has its radius widened from 25m to 40m, but its raw healing cut by 20–50% depending on level, and both healing passives stop granting flat healing amplification in favour of amplification that scales with attack power. Separately, the cross-class healing-received debuff is strengthened at the skill level while being softened in the Abyss and the Arena.

## UI improvements

### Closet

- The [Auto-register] count for synthesis expands from a maximum of 10 to a maximum of 30.
- The Closet Shop and its related features are removed. The cosmetics that used to be sold there move to the [Shop] → [Skin] tab.

### Shop

- The entrance cutscene that played when entering the shop is removed.
- The product list is redesigned as cards, with a banner shown at the top of the list.
- The product information screen becomes a full-screen UI.
- When a product includes a skin, wings, a pet or an emote, it can be previewed on the product information screen.
- A new [Skin] tab is added. The outfit sets, weapon skins and single cosmetic pieces that used to be sold in the Closet Shop are sold in the [Skin] tab.
- Products with a sale price of 0 now display 'Free' in the price information.

### Transparent Map

- The transparent map's lock state is kept when you quit the game and reconnect.
- A transparent-map move button is added, letting you drag the map to the position you want.
- Town and hunting-ground area markers are now displayed on the transparent map.

## Events

### fromis_9 collaboration ends (The Stolen Stage Props Incident)

- With the event over, the event quests can no longer be progressed.
- The NPCs and event-related objects placed in the towns are removed.
- The fromis_9-exclusive stages placed in each town revert to the normal stage appearance.
- Performing on the stage through interaction remains possible, but stage effects such as the fireworks no longer play.

### 'Expedition & Transcendence Reward Boost Up' ends

- With the event over, the related event items and material-conversion recipes are removed, and reward amounts return to their original values.

## Other improvements & fixes

### Improvements

- Morheim region: the NPC Oliver, who appears in the 'A Memento' quest, is renamed Ollie.
- Adjusted so that more territory duty quests appropriate to the character's level are shown.
- The Ishalgen server's abbreviated name changes to 'Halgen'.
- Nameplate Abyss rank: when a stealth skill is used, the Abyss rank icon is now shown in white for allies and red for enemies.
- The purchase-restriction conditions for wings and emotes listed in the Shop and in content coin shops now also take into account whether the item is held in the character warehouse.
- Improved so that 'My Skin' can still be selected to wear your own character's appearance while a full special outfit set that includes a skin is equipped. After equipping the whole special outfit set without hiding anything, selecting the chest piece activates the 'My Skin' / 'Set Skin' buttons.
- Pet auto-loot can now be toggled ON/OFF. Turning it OFF stops auto-loot, but the remaining usage time still counts down while it is off.
- The contribution needed to obtain the Odyle Energy Cube in Expedition and Transcendence is raised from 1% to 5%. This percentage may be adjusted again later depending on how it plays out.

### Fixes

- Character Switch: fixed soul-binding stones assigned to a quick slot being changed to high-grade soul-binding stones when switching characters through the Character Switch window.
- Material conversion: fixed the recipe changing, or the quantity you set being reset, on the first conversion after entering the material-conversion UI.

- Battlefield: the healing-received reduction and HP-potion recovery reduction debuffs applied on entering the battlefield are moved into the base stats that apply inside the battlefield. HP-potion recovery is unchanged, but healing received is reduced by a further 30% compared with before.
- Fixed floating monsters placed in the territories that switched into a flying state when struck, so that they no longer do so.
- Fixed the shortcut key not applying to the entry confirmation button when entering Shugo Festival and Dimensional Invasion.
- Fixed terrain in 'Chaotic Middle Reshanta' where a character could get stuck in mid-air.
- Fixed territory-transfer portal markers not being displayed on the world map.
- The amount of Silentium obtained from Hidden Cubes is slightly increased.
- 'Rampaging Fearsome Aura: Accessory (Soul-bound)' is added to the [Expedition Exchange Shop], which is purchased into by clearing Trial dungeons.

## Frequently asked questions

- What changed for healers in the AION 2 August 12, 2026 update?Almost every Cleric and Chanter heal has its radius widened from 25m to 40m while its healing output is cut by 20–50% depending on level, and several specs that boosted recovery below 50% HP or added HP-proportional healing are halved. Both healing passives — the Chanter's Blessing of Life and the Cleric's Healing Enhancement — lose their flat healing amplification entirely and instead grant amplification that scales with attack power, at 18/21/24% for the Chanter and 26/30/34% for the Cleric.
- What new instances open on August 12, 2026 in AION 2?Two. The Expedition Consumed Deus Research Base opens its Exploration and Conquest modes, requiring level 50 and item level 3,800 for Exploration and Conquest Normal, or 4,500 for Conquest Hard. The Transcendence Noiran's Hidden Legacy opens stages 1 through 4, scaling from item level 3,800 up to 4,800, and it is where the new Dice and Lantern Arcana parts drop.
- How does Battlefield matching work after the August 12 AION 2 patch?Teams are now built regardless of race, and you can only queue as a solo player or as a group of exactly 5. Groups of 5 are matched only against other groups of 5, while solo players are matched only against other solo players with a class-balance correction applied between the teams. Players who stay out of combat for a set length of time are forcibly removed from the battlefield.
- Is the fromis_9 collaboration still available in AION 2?No. The Stolen Stage Props Incident event ends with this update. Its quests can no longer be progressed, the event NPCs and objects are removed from the towns, and the fromis_9-exclusive stages revert to the normal stage appearance. You can still perform on those stages by interacting with them, but the fireworks and other stage effects no longer play.
- What is the new Draconic accessory tier in AION 2?Blueprints for Genesis Dragon Lord and Nemesis Dragon Lord accessories are added. They are crafted by using Splendent Noble Dragon Lord or Splendent Horned Dragon Lord accessories as inheritance material, and their crafting material — Rampaging Fearsome Aura: Accessory (Soul-bound) — comes from material-converting a Lava Heart accessory or the gear-change ticket from the new expedition's guaranteed rewards. On the Abyss side, Guardian and Archon Praetorian Captain accessories join the Abyss shop with an inheritance blueprint from the High Commander tier.
