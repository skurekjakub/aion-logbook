#
AION 2 – Best PvE Builds for Every Class

Find the best AION 2 build for every class, including the strongest skills, Stigmas, stat priorities, and PvE setups for all eight Global classes.

Ali Amir

October 6, 2026

All content for: AION 2

AION 2 gives every class enough skills and Stigmas to build around different playstyles, but you should not spread your Skill Points evenly across everything. The strongest PvE builds focus investment into the abilities that define each class, then support them with the right passives and four Stigmas.

This guide covers a reliable PvE and general progression build for all eight Global classes. These setups work well for leveling, Expeditions, Conquest, Transcendence, and general endgame progression, while Templar and Cleric keep the tanking and healing tools their groups actually need.

## 2. Best Builds for All AION 2 Classes

These builds prioritize the abilities that make the biggest difference to each class rather than trying to level every skill at the same rate.

### 2.1. Gladiator – Rending Blow Build

Keen Strike Gladiator Build

The best Gladiator PvE setup revolves around Rending Blow and Overhead Slam. Rending Blow gives you reliable multi-target damage, while Overhead Slam becomes much stronger once its higher-level Specialties remove the cooldown restrictions that normally slow the rotation down.

Ruinous Blow is another major priority because it grants Prepare for Battle, increasing your PvE damage during the buff window. Gladiator can also naturally survive on the frontline, so most of your remaining investment can go toward increasing damage instead of building entirely around defense.

- Core Skills: Rending Blow, Overhead Slam, Ruinous Blow, Sword Aura Rampage

- Best Passives: Experienced Counterstrike, Attack Preparation, Impact Hit, Murderous Burst

- Recommended Stigmas: Rage Burst, Lunge Stance, Zikel’s Blessing, Focused Block

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Critical Hit → Double → Attack / Damage Boost

How to play: Activate Ruinous Blow and your offensive buffs before your main damage window, then use Rage Burst to open repeated Overhead Slam attacks while cycling Rending Blow between them. Keep Focused Block available when you expect heavy incoming damage.

### 2.2. Templar – Judgment Tank Build

Punishment Skill Templar Build

Templar should be built around Judgment, its shield attacks, and enough survivability to maintain boss aggro. Judgment is repeatedly triggered through several of your shield abilities, making it one of the central attacks in the class’s PvE rotation.

Punishment is another major skill because its Executor buff increases PvE damage, while Warding Strike gives both healing and defensive value. Your goal is not simply to survive—you should maintain Enmity while continuing to contribute damage and party utility.

- Core Skills: Judgment, Punishment, Shield Smite, Warding Strike, Vicious Strike

- Best Passives: Fury, Ironclad Defense, Insulting Roar, Enhance Health

- Recommended Stigmas: Battlefield Banner, Doom Shield, Taunt, Empyrean Lord’s Punishment

- Defensive Swap: Use Nezekan’s Shield when the party needs additional protection.

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Combat Speed → Attack / Damage Boost → Block

How to play: Keep the boss facing away from your party, maintain Enmity with your normal rotation and Taunt when required, then cycle shield skills to trigger Judgment. Use Punishment during your damage window and save defensive abilities for mechanics that could threaten the group.

### 2.3. Assassin – Heart Gore Build

Assassin

The Assassin’s strongest PvE setup revolves around Heart Gore, Critical Hits, and Insignia stacks. Heart Gore becomes available after landing Critical Hits, while Insignia Explosion rewards you for building several Insignia stacks before detonating them.

The class becomes much stronger once you can reliably stay behind bosses. Shadowstrike and Ambush help with positioning, while passives such as Exploit Weakness and Rear Smite increase the value of Critical Hits and Back Attacks.

- Core Skills: Heart Gore, Insignia Explosion, Savage Roar, Quick Slice, Ambush

- Best Passives: Exploit Weakness, Rear Smite, Assault Stance, Impact Hit

- Recommended Stigmas: Savage Fang, Triniel’s Dagger, Illusive Clone, Swift Contract

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Critical Hit → Back Attack Damage → Critical Damage / Weapon Damage

How to play: Build Insignias with Savage Roar and Savage Fang, stay behind the boss whenever possible, then use Insignia Explosion at high stacks. Combine Illusive Clone and Swift Contract during burst windows so you can trigger Heart Gore repeatedly at high speed.

### 2.4. Ranger – Deadshot Build

Snipe Skill Ranger Build

The Ranger build revolves around keeping Gale and Precision active while repeatedly using Deadshot and your other ranged attacks. Gale Arrow increases Combat Speed and PvE Damage Boost, while Marking Shot grants Precision and improves Critical Hit.

Deadshot receives additional damage while Precision is active, so you should avoid firing it without setting up the buff first. Snare Shot is also important because several Ranger attacks gain extra value against Slowed or Rooted enemies.

- Core Skills: Deadshot, Gale Arrow, Drill Dart, Snipe, Marking Shot, Snare Shot

- Best Passives: Focused Eye, Hunter’s Resolve, Hunter’s Soul, Rooting Eye

- Recommended Stigmas: Vaizel’s Authority, Bow of Blessing, Supporting Fire, Griffon Arrow

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Critical Hit → Attack → Double / Damage Boost

How to play: Maintain Marking Shot for Precision, keep Gale Arrow’s buff active, then use Deadshot during those buff windows. Apply Snare Shot before attacks that gain bonuses against slowed targets, and keep your distance rather than trading damage at close range.

### 2.5. Sorcerer – Hellfire Build

Hellfire Skill Sorcerer Build

Sorcerer deals its best PvE damage by cycling Hellfire, Firestorm, Blaze, and Fire-related buffs. Hellfire provides the heavy burst, while Firestorm becomes especially important once its Specialties start reducing Hellfire’s cooldown.

MP management is part of the build because Grace of Enhancement provides a large damage increase while your MP remains above its required threshold. This makes MP-restoring skills and reduced MP costs more valuable than they initially appear.

- Core Skills: Hellfire, Firestorm, Blaze, Flame Arrow, Wish of Concentration

- Best Passives: Grace of Enhancement, Robe of Flame, Fire Mark, Robe of Earth

- Recommended Stigmas: Element Enhancement, Delayed Explosion, Fire Wall, Cold Storm

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Cooldown Reduction → Combat Speed → Attack → Critical Hit

How to play: Activate Element Enhancement before your burst sequence, maintain your Fire effects, then use Firestorm to bring Hellfire back faster. Avoid draining your MP completely because dropping too low reduces the value of several important Sorcerer passives.

### 2.6. Spiritmaster – Elemental Fusion Build

Spiritmaster

Spiritmaster relies on its summoned spirits to build toward Elemental Fusion. Each spirit provides a different type of utility: Water is strong for single-target situations, Fire is useful against groups, Earth provides tanking utility, and Wind offers additional sustain.

The main damage loop comes from keeping a spirit active, maintaining Jointstrike: Curse and other damage-over-time effects, and generating Four Elements so you can repeatedly activate Elemental Fusion.

- Core Skills: Elemental Fusion, Jointstrike: Curse, Dimensional Control, Combustion, Spirit Summons

- Best Passives: Spirit Strike, Spirit Descent, Consecutive Countercurrent, Elemental Unification

- Recommended Stigmas: Summon: Ancient Spirit, Flame Blessing, Jointstrike: Destructive Attack, Jointstrike: Corrode

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Combat Speed → Attack → Critical Hit → Double / Damage Boost

How to play: Use the spirit that fits the encounter, keep Jointstrike: Curse active, and build Four Elements as quickly as possible. Use Summon: Ancient Spirit during major damage windows because its attacks help generate more Elemental Fusion activations.

### 2.7. Cleric – Hybrid Healer Build

Heal Block Skill Cleric Build

Cleric does not need to stand still and spam healing for an entire dungeon. Its strongest general PvE setup combines reliable healing with Chain of Torment and Condemnation, allowing you to reduce enemy PvE Damage Tolerance and contribute damage whenever the party is safe.

Healing Light should still receive major investment because it is your most reliable short-cooldown heal. Radiant Recovery and other support skills remain available for situations where the whole group needs healing or cleansing.

- Core Skills: Healing Light, Chain of Torment, Condemnation, Judgment Thunder, Radiant Recovery

- Best Passives: Healing Enhancement, Empyrean Lords’ Grace, Earth’s Grace, Warm Benediction

- Recommended Stigmas: Earth’s Punishment, Light of Protection, Noble Aura, Resurrection

- Safer Progression Option: Keep Resurrection equipped until your group consistently clears mechanics.

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Combat Speed → Critical Hit → Attack → Double / Damage Boost

How to play: Keep Chain of Torment on the boss to improve the party’s damage, follow it with Condemnation, and fill downtime with offensive skills. Stop attacking immediately when healing is required—your first job in difficult group content is still keeping the party alive.

### 2.8. Chanter – Dark Crush Build

Chanter

Chanter combines melee damage with some of the strongest group buffs available to a support class. The build revolves around repeatedly creating opportunities to use Dark Crush through Spinning Strike, Impactful Crush, and other attacks.

Spinning Strike should be one of your highest priorities because it activates Dark Crush and provides a stacking Critical Damage Boost. Chanter also brings valuable party buffs through its Stigmas, allowing it to increase the group’s overall damage while continuing to attack.

- Core Skills: Dark Crush, Spinning Strike, Impactful Crush, Incandescent Blow, Onslaught

- Best Passives: Attack Preparation, Earth’s Promise, Impact Hit, Inspiring Spell

- Recommended Stigmas: Undefeated Mantra, Power of the Storm, Marchutan’s Wrath, Fracturing Blow

- Support Skill: Keep Recuperation available for emergency healing and cleansing.

- Stat Priority: Accuracy → Combat Speed → Attack → Critical Hit → Damage Boost / Double

How to play: Use Spinning Strike and Impactful Crush to keep opening Dark Crush windows, then activate Dark Crush whenever it becomes available. Save Power of the Storm and Fracturing Blow for major party burst windows instead of using every buff as soon as its cooldown ends.

## 3. Which AION 2 Build Should You Play?

Ranger

If you want straightforward solo progression, Gladiator and Ranger are among the easiest builds to use effectively. Assassin offers stronger burst but requires better positioning, while Sorcerer and Spiritmaster provide safer ranged damage with more resource or summon management.

For group content, Templar is the dedicated tank, Cleric is the main healer, and Chanter is the strongest choice if you enjoy supporting the party while still dealing meaningful damage. Whichever class you choose, prioritize its core skills and Stigmas first instead of trying to upgrade every ability equally.
