# Cleric guide

Mace-wielding main healer and the only class that can resurrect an ally. Heals at 40 m (Radiant Recovery, Healing Light, Light of Regeneration), buffs the party (Light of Protection, Earth’s Blessing) and deals real damage at 20 m between heals through the Chain of Torment → Condemnation loop. Easy to pick up, demanding in responsibility: in a dungeon, if the Cleric falls, nobody can raise them.

## At a glance

### Strengths

- +The game’s only class resurrection (Summon Resurrection, 40 m): a guaranteed spot in every party and raid.
- +Instant heals, castable while moving and at 40 m, with built-in cleansing (Radiant Recovery, Absolution, Benevolence at tier 20).
- +Big party multiplier: Light of Protection (10.5 to 20% PvE Damage Boost and Tolerance), Earth’s Blessing, Yustiel’s Power, plus the Chain of Torment and Debilitating Mark debuffs.
- +Real damage between heals: on a target afflicted with Earth Punishment, Condemnation always crits and loses its cooldown.
- +Self-sufficient solo and while leveling: 20 m attacks, self-heals, and Radiant Benediction healing while you hit.

### Weaknesses

- −Fragile in melee: no dedicated dodge, only Defiance (60 s) and Salvation (stigma).
- −Cannot raise itself on the global version: its death often loses the fight.
- −Only 4 stigma slots at the global launch to cover healing, damage and resurrection: every content forces a choice.
- −Weak in duels against burst melee classes (Gladiator, Assassin, Templar) and kiters (Ranger), according to Korean players.
- −Demanding damage loop: Condemnation needs Chain of Torment on the target, and keeping it up requires a lot of cooldown reduction.

### Mechanics to know

- Basic attack: Earth’s Retribution (left click, 20 m) restores 110 MP; its follow-ups Thunder and Lightning (110 MP) and Discharge (150 MP, random trigger) make it your mana source.
- Damage loop: Chain of Torment (20 s) marks the target and Condemnation (3 s) can only hit a marked target. Condemnation’s level-12 spec resets its cooldown on a crit; Earth Punishment (tier 5) makes its hits critical, so Condemnation loses its cooldown.
- Judgment Thunder (right click, no cooldown) triggers Divine Punishment; the level-16 spec adds one activation.
- Heals: Healing Light (6 s, 1 cast, 3 with the spec) targets you and the lowest ally; Radiant Recovery (12 s) heals and cleanses the party within 40 m; Light of Regeneration heals the party for 30 s on a 30 s cooldown.
- Heals cannot crit; Healing Enhancement turns part of your Attack into Heal Boost: Attack raises both your heals and your damage.
- Light of Protection is a toggle (5 s cooldown) to keep on; it does not stack with the Chanter’s Undefeated Mantra (the higher level applies, the Chanter’s on a tie).
- Earth Punishment grants the party Earth’s Blessing (tier 10: Double Chance; tier 15: Attack and Defense); it is not received while the Chanter’s Power of the Storm is active (global client tooltip).
- Noble Aura (stigma) lasts 300 s and attacks every 2 s (1 s at tier 20); it disappears when you die.
- Lightning Strike Scattershot only targets a staggered enemy: charged Bolt (20 to 35 stagger), Power Burst (50), Earth Punishment (20) and Root (15) fill the gauge.
- Defiance removes Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost and Fear, then grants 5 s of Tenacity; its 60 s cooldown is not affected by cooldown reduction.
- Progression (global client): at most one skill level every 3 character levels; specializations at 8, 12 and 16 (third slot at level 20); Earth’s Grace (21), Survival Willpower (23) and Radiant Benediction (25) can be raised to 10 as soon as they are learned.
- Stigmas at level 22, 4 slots on the global version, level 20 cap: 75 shards per stigma (1, 2, 4 then 8 per level), tiers at 5, 10, 15 and 20.

Leveling 1 to 45Medium reliability

### Leveling 1–45: self-sufficient striker, damage first

You level by hitting: Earth’s Retribution and Judgment Thunder maxed, then the Chain of Torment → Condemnation loop from level 8. Heals only keep you alive between packs, and Radiant Benediction (25) heals while you attack.

#### Tips

- Use Target mode (AION 1) while leveling: basic attacks keep going between skills and restore MP.
- Put Chain of Torment and Condemnation on the same hotbar line, Chain first: Condemnation only fires on a marked target.
- Don’t pull more than 4 monsters: your AoE skills hit 4 targets at most.
- Do the side quests: they give Daevanion points (boards at 12, 20, 30 and 40).
- Points run short at 45 (203 from levels, 383 with the regional quests and sealed dungeons; the Monolith no longer gives any): don’t raise everything to 10. At a fresh 45: Condemnation, Earth’s Retribution, Bolt, Divine Aura, Chain of Torment and Judgment Thunder at 10, the three heals and Debilitating Mark at 8, then Empyrean Lord’s Grace at 10 and Earth’s Grace at 4: 202 points, this build’s targets (aion2maps, Oct 2).
- At 45, reset (it is free) your points and Stigmas for your activity’s build: dungeon healer, with or without a Chanter, offensive as a second Cleric, solo farming; keep each set on its own preset, each has its own macro.

#### Tips

- Stay 20–25 m from the boss: your attacks reach 20 m and your heals 40 m.
- Don’t die: no other class can resurrect, and you cannot raise yourself on the global version.
- If a Chanter joins, switch to the “with a Chanter” build: Light of Protection and Undefeated Mantra do not stack.
- Recast Noble Aura after every death or teleport: it disappears with you.
- In easy expeditions (Krao Cave, Draupnir), healing is light: hit more (MMO Codex).
- Chest contribution: 5% of the final boss's HP in damage, or 20% of the party's damage healed according to GamersTogether (Aug 15); Kaeria (Oct 3) warns that a Cleric who only heals can leave with nothing: hit as soon as the party is stable.
- Endgame: weapon and guard to +10 first, then bracelets; armor and jewelry at +5 are enough early on. The best global Clerics have the weapon at +10 for 63%, the guard at +10 for 56%, bracelets at +7 on average, armor and jewelry around +4 to +5 (MetaBot.GG, Oct 2).

## PvP

The class's role in PvP and how to approach each opponent.

A group healer above all: excellent in 5v5 and in group Abyss (heals, cleanses, Salvation, resurrection outside the arena), weak in duels. In 1v1, it wins by attrition (debuffs, Condemnation, heals) but struggles against burst melee classes and kiters (Korean opinions, December 2025 to April 2026). In Korea, the healing-received penalty in the Abyss and the arena went from 30% to 20% on Aug 12.

### Against each class

- TemplarThreat
  - They can counter everything: pull, stuns and blocks (DC Inside, Dec 2025).
  - Stay out of range of their pull; keep Defiance for the stun that precedes their burst.
  - Voice of Doom (−60% healing, shields dispelled at tier 10) against their heals and shields.
- GladiatorThreat
  - “Natural disaster” for Korean Clerics: never stay within range of their gap-closers.
  - Root as soon as they approach (75%, 100% at tier 15), then back off to 20 m.
  - Salvation on their Rage Burst, Defiance for their knockdowns.
- AssassinThreat
  - Their stealth opener can kill you in 1 to 2 s (Inven): keep Salvation and Defiance for it.
  - Survive the opener, then Root and heals: over time, the advantage comes back to the Cleric.
  - Yustiel's Power (10% shield) before entering a risky zone.
- RangerThreat
  - “Impossible to beat” for a Korean Cleric (DC Inside, Dec 2025): they kite you from range.
  - Stick to obstacles and force the fight within 20 m.
  - Cleanse their slows and immobilizations with Radiant Recovery.
- SorcererThreat
  - A matter of timing: Salvation before their transformation or big spell (DC Inside).
  - Defiance removes Frost and Fear.
- SpiritmasterThreat
  - Built around evasion, they are very hard to kill (DC Inside, Dec 2025): the “Ignores Block and Evasion” specs of Chain of Torment and Debilitating Mark help.
  - Defiance against their Fear; cleanse their Corrosion (−70% healing).
- ClericThreat
  - Mirror: attrition fight; Voice of Doom (−60% healing and potions) often decides it.
  - Save Salvation for after theirs.
- ChanterThreat
  - Both Korean camps agree: the Cleric is at a disadvantage (guaranteed crits, cleansing of your debuffs, the Chanter's stamina; Inven 26407).
  - Voice of Doom against their healing; don't waste Defiance on their first stun.

### PvP tips

- Separate skill preset for PvP (different stigmas and macro).
- Defiance (60 s, cannot be reduced): keep it for the control that precedes a burst, not for the first one that comes along.
- Salvation (3 s of immunity, 150 s): at tier 15 it also protects the lowest ally, at tier 20 it triggers on its own under an Impact-type status.
- Survival Willpower (23, raised to 10 right away): Status Effect Resist +17%, the priority PvP passive.
- In the Arena of Cooperation (1 life), Summon Resurrection is useless: take Salvation or Absolution.
- Voice of Doom (−60% healing and potions for 20 s) against healers and self-healing classes.

## In a group

What the class brings to a group and the pairings that work.

### What it brings

- +The only class resurrection: Summon Resurrection revives dead allies within 40 m (120 s cooldown; tier 10: 3 s of invincibility).
- +Party heals within 40 m with cleansing: Radiant Recovery (12 s), Light of Regeneration (30 s heal over time, Tolerance +5% with spec 16), Benevolence, Absolution (cleanses everything at tier 10).
- +Light of Protection: PvE Damage Boost and Tolerance of 10.5% (up to 20% at level 20) for the party, Max HP +10% at tier 15; does not stack with Undefeated Mantra.
- +Earth's Blessing (Earth Punishment): Double +5% (tier 10), then Attack and Defense +5% (tier 15) for the party for 10 s (20 s at tier 20); blocked by Power of the Storm.
- +Boss debuffs: Chain of Torment (PvE Damage Tolerance −5%, −15% with spec 12) and Debilitating Mark (PvE Damage Boost −5%, −15% with spec 12; Defense −15% with spec 8).
- +Yustiel's Power: PvE Damage Tolerance +20% (30% at tier 20) and a shield of 10% HP for 10 s for the party.
- +Stagger: Bolt 20 to 35, Power Burst 50, Earth Punishment 20, Root 15.

### What it needs from the group

- ›A Templar who holds the boss: you heal and hit from 20–40 m.
- ›A Chanter for their Sprint Mantra (speed and stamina) and their Healing Touch as backup; coordinate Light of Protection ↔ Undefeated Mantra and Earth Punishment ↔ Power of the Storm.
- ›Allies within 40 m: all your party heals have that range.
- ›Someone to protect you: without you, no more resurrection.

### Pairings

- Chanter“호치” duo: the Chanter buffs and hits, you heal. Light of Protection and Undefeated Mantra do not stack; Earth's Blessing is not received during Power of the Storm: recast Earth Punishment right after. Ask them for Sprint Mantra. Their Earth's Promise does not apply when used together with your Chain of Torment (global client tooltip).
- TemplarThey hold the boss and take the big hits: Healing Light always targets the lowest ally, keep Radiant Recovery for their debuffs.
- GladiatorDurable melee (drains) hitting from the front, next to the tank: Light of Regeneration and Radiant Recovery cover both.
- AssassinFragile burst: keep Healing Light for them after their openers; Earth's Blessing (Double) benefits their burst.
- SorcererBahamut (TW, Nov 2025) pairs the Sorcerer with the Cleric in the best 4-player composition; stay between the melee and them to cover everyone within 40 m.
- RangerRanged DPS: position yourself between the melee and them; your 40 m cover both.

## Common mistakes

The costliest mistakes, gathered from guides and players.

1. 1Putting Light of Protection in the macro: it is a toggle, the loop switches it off.
2. 2Casting Condemnation without Chain of Torment on the target: it won't fire.
3. 3Casting Earth Punishment during Power of the Storm: Earth's Blessing is not received.
4. 4Keeping Light of Protection with a Chanter without coordinating: one of the two buffs is lost.
5. 5Forgetting to recast Noble Aura after a death or a teleport.
6. 6Dying: nobody else can resurrect, and the global version has no self-resurrection.
7. 7Using Lightning Strike Scattershot outside stagger: it only fires on a staggered target.
8. 8Stacking Critical Hit to heal: heals don't crit.
9. 9Copying a Korean set with 5–6 stigmas or a Daevanion order with 8 boards: the global version has 4 slots and 5 boards.
10. 10Raising every skill to 10 while leveling: each costs 21 points and you only have 203 at 45 (383 with the regional quests and sealed dungeons).
11. 11Wasting Absolution on a minor wound (90 s cooldown).
12. 12Casting Yustiel's Power after the big hit: it reduces incoming damage, it doesn't heal.
13. 13Taking Summon Resurrection in the Arena of Cooperation (1 life).
14. 14Using external mouse macros: penalized in Korea, risky on the global servers.
15. 15Staying in melee with the boss: your attacks reach 20 m and your heals 40 m.
16. 16Following a guide older than Apr 22 (Korea): healing, Light of Protection and Noble Aura were reworked.
