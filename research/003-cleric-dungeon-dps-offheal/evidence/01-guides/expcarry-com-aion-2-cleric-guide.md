# AION 2 Cleric Guide

ExpCarry Editorial Team

Updated: 06 Oct 202625 min Read time27351 Views

The AION 2 Cleric is the main dedicated healer in the live Global version, but the class is not designed to stand idle while waiting for party HP to drop. Cleric uses a Mace and Guard and combines party healing, cleansing, protection, battle resurrection and ranged magical damage. Healing Enhancement also converts part of Cleric's Attack scaling into stronger healing, so offensive progression and support performance are closely connected.

Short answer: build Condemnation, Radiant Recovery, Bolt, Divine Aura, Judgment Thunder and Healing Light. For standard PvE, the default four-Stigma package is Light of Protection, Earth Punishment, Noble Aura and Prayer of Amplification. Chain of Torment enables Condemnation, Earth Punishment makes Condemnation critically hit its affected target, and Condemnation can reset its own cooldown on a Critical Hit. Heal deliberately, then return to the Condemnation loop whenever the party is stable.

## How Does Cleric Work in AION 2?

Cleric is the primary healing class and uses a Mace with a Guard. The live Global kit combines direct recovery, healing over time, cleansing, party buffs, defensive Stigmas, battle resurrection and Earth- and Wind-based ranged damage.

The important Global playstyle difference is that Cleric is not a passive heal bot. Heal when the party needs it, pre-heal predictable damage and then return to offensive skills as soon as the group is stable. Radiant Benediction even restores party HP while offensive attacks land, reinforcing this hybrid playstyle.

Healing Enhancement makes Attack part of the healing build. There is no need to abandon useful offensive progression simply because Cleric is the healer.

### Understanding Cleric's Healing Mechanics

Cleric's recovery skills solve different problems and should not be treated as interchangeable buttons.

- Healing Light: fast direct recovery for Cleric and an eligible nearby party member with low HP. Developed Specialties add consecutive uses and stronger healing.
- Radiant Recovery: group recovery plus removal of a removable debuff. At a developed level it gains an additional use, lower cooldown and stronger recovery.
- Light of Regeneration: sustained healing over time for repeated incoming damage. Use it proactively rather than as a substitute for emergency burst healing.
- Healing Enhancement: converts Attack scaling into stronger healing output.
- Radiant Benediction: restores nearby party members while Cleric continues landing attacks.

Healing should follow encounter priority instead of a fixed rotation. Saving the correct response for a dangerous mechanic is more valuable than pressing every heal immediately after minor damage.

### Cleric Strengths and Weaknesses

Cleric becomes more valuable as group content becomes more demanding because healing, cleansing and resurrection solve problems that raw personal DPS cannot.

| Content | Strengths | Main weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boss PvE | Strong healing, cleansing, resurrection, party buffs and useful damage between mechanics | Poor MP management or slow reactions can still cause a wipe. |
| Solo PvE | Self-recovery and ranged damage make progression forgiving | Clear speed falls if too much of the build is sacrificed for defensive support. |
| Arena / 1v1 | Recovery and defensive cooldowns can outlast an opponent's first burst | Cleric is often focused first and has lower kill pressure than a dedicated DPS. |
| Small-scale PvP | Strong ally stabilization, cleansing and protection | Coordinated pressure can force several defensive cooldowns together. |
| Mass PvP | Backline healing, cleansing, protection and resurrection are extremely valuable | Bad positioning removes access to allies and exposes the healer to focus. |

The best Cleric is not simply the player with the largest raw healing total. Correct pre-healing, useful cleanses, keeping party buffs active, preserving MP and contributing damage during safe windows all matter.

## Best Cleric Skills and Specialties

Global active-skill Specialty slots open at effective skill levels 8, 12 and 20. Individual Specialty effects can unlock at level 16, but the third equipped Specialty slot still requires level 20. Mastery, Daevanion, rings and Arcana all contribute to displayed skill levels.

Normal skills naturally progress to their base ceiling, while the four PvE Daevanion boards and ring skill lines provide important external levels. Check the final displayed level before investing extra resources.

| Radiant Recovery | 20 | Additional use, cooldown reduction, stronger healing | Core party heal and cleanse. |
| Bolt | 20 | Skill Speed, stronger single-target damage, guaranteed Critical Hit | Charge manually, especially during Stagger. |

### Which Cleric Specialties Matter Most?

Specialties should support the selected content rather than simply taking the largest visible coefficient.

- Condemnation: cooldown reset on Critical Hit is the core of the live damage loop. Multi-Hit becomes valuable when the third slot opens.
- Radiant Recovery: additional use and lower cooldown improve both normal healing and emergency recovery.
- Healing Light: consecutive uses are particularly useful when one ally repeatedly takes damage.
- Bolt: faster charge and guaranteed Critical Hit make it strong during Stagger and burst windows.
- Divine Aura: higher attack speed, longer duration and cooldown reduction improve its passive contribution.
- Earth's Retribution: Discharge-related effects improve both MP recovery and Bolt access.

Do not confuse purchased skill levels with the displayed level after Daevanion, rings and Arcana. External levels can complete an important breakpoint without additional base investment.

### Where Should You Spend Cleric Skill Points?

The live Global build should establish the damage engine and healing core together.

1. Build Condemnation first: it is the center of Cleric's boss damage.
2. Establish Healing Light and Radiant Recovery: these must remain reliable before difficult group content.
3. Develop Bolt, Divine Aura and Judgment Thunder: they form the strongest secondary damage package.
4. Maintain Chain of Torment: Condemnation depends on its target condition.
5. Use Daevanion and ring levels efficiently: important actives can reach level 20 without relying entirely on Arcana.
6. Check the final displayed level: avoid paying for levels that do not cross a useful threshold.

A dungeon Cleric should not sacrifice the healing core for a small theoretical damage gain, but overinvesting in healing after the party is already safe also wastes progression.

## Build 1: Best Cleric Boss Healer Build

The live Global boss-healer setup is a hybrid healer/DPS build. Heal deliberately, stabilize the party and then return to the Condemnation loop instead of standing idle.

Core healing skills: Healing Light, Radiant Recovery and Light of Regeneration.

Core offensive skills: Condemnation, Chain of Torment, Earth's Retribution, Judgment Thunder, Divine Aura, Bolt and Debilitating Mark.

Passive priorities: Empyrean Lord's Grace, Earth's Grace, Healing Enhancement and Radiant Benediction.

Standard Stigma direction: Light of Protection, Earth Punishment, Noble Aura and Prayer of Amplification. In a party with Chanter, replace Light of Protection when Undefeated Mantra already covers the overlapping party effect.

### Cleric Healing Priority for Boss Fights

Healing follows a priority system instead of a fixed rotation.

1. Before predictable damage: prepare Light of Regeneration and use Yustiel's Power if that Stigma is equipped.
2. Several players injured or debuffed: use Radiant Recovery because it combines group healing with cleansing.
3. One ally in danger: use Healing Light and its consecutive uses.
4. Emergency: use Absolution or another equipped emergency Stigma when normal recovery is not enough.
5. After a death: use Summon Resurrection only when the cast can be completed safely.
6. During stable periods: immediately return to Condemnation and offensive debuffs.

Important: do not spend every major recovery cooldown after harmless party damage. Keep the correct answer for the next dangerous mechanic.

### Cleric Damage Rotation Between Healing Windows

When the party is stable, Cleric should immediately contribute damage.

1. Keep Light of Protection active when no higher-priority Chanter effect replaces it.
2. Apply Debilitating Mark.
3. Apply Chain of Torment.
4. Apply Earth Punishment.
5. Use Condemnation repeatedly while Critical Hits continue resetting it.
6. Keep Judgment Thunder, Divine Aura and Earth's Retribution flowing between Condemnation inputs.
7. Fully charge Bolt during a good Stagger or burst window, preferably with Prayer of Amplification active.
8. Stop immediately when healing or mechanics take priority.

Renew Chain of Torment and Earth Punishment when they expire. Do not allow a long offensive animation to delay a required heal.

## Cleric Boss Guide: Mechanics, Healing, and Loot

The live Global article should not import fixed Korean or Taiwanese boss scripts as universal facts. Boss tuning can differ between regions and difficulties, so use live telegraphs as authoritative.

The reward notes below are progression guidance, not guarantees that one named boss always gives one specific personal drop.

### Krao Cave

Krao Cave contains Neglected Gadioton, Enhanced Harcon and Ultimate Berk. For Cleric, movement comes before healing through a lethal mechanic.

| Boss | Main focus | What Cleric should do | Reward check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neglected Gadioton | Ground and directed telegraphs | Keep regeneration prepared but leave danger before committing to a longer cast. | Check the current Global activity reward pool. |
| Enhanced Harcon | Repeated movement | Use short heals while repositioning and resume damage once the party stabilizes. | Compare Mace and Guard upgrades by Soul Binding and useful stats. |
| Ultimate Berk | Movement and Stagger | Prepare healing before heavy sequences and preserve Bolt or Scattershot when assigned to Stagger. | Use the live difficulty reward preview rather than old regional BiS lists. |

Do not try to heal players through a mechanic that requires correct positioning. Move first, then recover the group.

Bolt and Lightning Strike Scattershot can contribute to Stagger when required, but do not spend them immediately before a mandatory check.

### Fire Temple

Fire Temple contains Red Spark Ignus, Black Smoke Murute and Kromede's Desire. The main healer mistake is spending too much MP on avoidable ground damage.

| Boss | Main focus | What Cleric should do | Reward check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red Spark Ignus | Persistent ground damage and movement | Pre-HoT repeated damage, but reposition instead of trying to heal through a bad location. | Fire Temple provides useful Mace and armor progression. |
| Black Smoke Murute | Overlapping attack sequences | Do not lock yourself into a long cast before the full movement pattern ends. | Check current accessories and material rewards. |
| Kromede's Desire | Downtime and movement phases | Do not waste Prayer of Amplification or other short offensive windows immediately before downtime. | Enraged Kromede Warhammer can be a stepping-stone weapon, but Soul Binding capacity matters. |

Kromede's movement phases are not an excuse to stand still and spam healing. Reach safety first and recover the group afterward.

Difficulty-specific regional mechanics should not be treated as guaranteed Global mechanics unless the live encounter shows them.

### Draupnir

Draupnir combines movement, ground pressure, debuffs and Stagger responsibilities. Cleric should preserve enough emergency recovery to stabilize the group after mandatory movement.

| Boss | Main focus | What Cleric should do | Reward check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Predator Saraswati | Ground hazards and removable effects | Cleanse when the effect is removable, but do not encourage the party to remain inside avoidable damage. | Evaluate Mace, Guard and accessories through the live reward preview. |
| Phantasmal Lakshmi | Movement and overlapping party damage | Keep Defiance ready for dangerous control and heal after the party reaches safety. | Compare Soul Binding and useful stats rather than only Item Level. |
| Transcendent Bakarma | Movement and coordinated Stagger | Keep emergency healing available and contribute charged Bolt or Scattershot only when assigned. | Check the current Conquest difficulty reward pool. |

Movement requirements take priority over casting another heal. Once the party reaches a safe position, Radiant Recovery and sustained healing can stabilize several injured allies quickly.

Summon Resurrection is valuable only when the death is recoverable. It cannot fix a failed mandatory group mechanic by itself.

## Build 2: Cleric AoE and Solo Farming

Solo Cleric uses the same offensive engine that fills downtime in group PvE. Self-healing makes the class forgiving, but oversized pulls that force constant recovery are inefficient.

Core skills: Condemnation, Chain of Torment, Judgment Thunder, Divine Aura, Bolt, Earth's Retribution and Debilitating Mark.

Important passives: Empyrean Lord's Grace, Earth's Grace, Healing Enhancement and Radiant Benediction.

Offensive Stigmas: Earth Punishment, Noble Aura and Prayer of Amplification form the core. Light of Protection is the normal fourth slot when no other utility is needed.

### Recommended Solo Damage Rotation

Use the offensive skills as one connected package rather than randomly pressing whatever is available.

1. Apply Debilitating Mark when the target will live long enough to benefit from it.
2. Apply Chain of Torment.
3. Apply Earth Punishment.
4. Run the Condemnation reset loop.
5. Use Divine Aura and Judgment Thunder for additional damage.
6. Use Earth's Retribution to restore MP and trigger Discharge.
7. Fully charge Bolt when the target and positioning allow it.

Against grouped enemies, position Divine Aura and Judgment Thunder so their multi-target value is not wasted.

### How Many Enemies Should Cleric Pull?

Self-healing allows larger pulls, but surviving a pack does not automatically make it efficient.

Stop increasing pull size once healing begins replacing most offensive actions. A smaller group killed continuously is better than a large pull followed by MP recovery downtime.

Earth's Retribution is important during repeated farming because its Discharge chain helps restore MP.

## Build 3: Defensive Cleric PvE

The defensive preset is intended for new encounters and unstable groups. It should solve the current survival problem without removing every offensive tool.

Keep Healing Light, Radiant Recovery and Light of Regeneration available, then adjust the four Stigma slots toward Absolution, Yustiel's Power and Summon Resurrection as required.

### When to Use Defensive Abilities

- Defiance: reserve it for dangerous control or when immediately regaining control prevents additional damage.
- Radiant Recovery: use it when group recovery and cleansing are both valuable.
- Absolution: use it for stronger emergency party recovery and cleansing.
- Yustiel's Power: activate it before a major predictable hit to increase party Damage Tolerance.
- Summon Resurrection: use it only when the revive cast is safe.
- Salvation: use it as personal emergency protection in appropriate content rather than as a way to ignore wipe mechanics.

Do not remove every offensive Stigma simply because an encounter is difficult. Longer fights create more opportunities for mistakes.

A defensive Cleric should still preserve MP, maintain essential debuffs and contribute to Stagger when assigned.

## Build 4: Cleric Arena PvP

In Arena, Cleric becomes a survival-oriented support combatant. Defensive timing, positioning and resource management become more important than maintaining the perfect PvE Condemnation loop.

Core skills: Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, Defiance, Earth's Retribution, Chain of Torment, Condemnation and Judgment Thunder.

Stigma direction: Salvation, Voice of Doom or other control, Absolution and Yustiel's Power are relevant PvP choices according to the matchup.

### How to Play Cleric in Arena

1. Maintain distance without moving outside your own useful attack and healing range.
2. Apply offensive pressure only while the enemy is not forcing a defensive response.
3. Reserve Defiance for meaningful control.
4. Use Salvation or Yustiel's Power around real burst windows.
5. Recover, reposition and return to damage after important enemy cooldowns are spent.
6. Do not commit to a fully charged Bolt if doing so exposes you to unavoidable burst.

Control effects are most valuable when they create time to heal, reposition or force the enemy to spend a defensive answer.

Long PvP fights can become resource contests, so MP discipline remains important.

## Build 5: Cleric Abyss and Mass PvP

Large-scale PvP makes Cleric a priority backline target. Positioning and survival come before personal damage.

The foundation remains Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, Light of Regeneration and the main healing passives, with Stigmas adjusted toward protection, cleansing and resurrection.

### Mass PvP Healing Priorities

- Before engagement: position behind the frontline and keep allies inside practical healing range.
- During the initial push: prepare sustained healing and party protection before the damage lands.
- When several allies are debuffed: cleanse when removing the effect solves the real problem.
- When focused: reposition and use personal defensives instead of dragging enemy pressure through other support players.
- After the engagement: restore MP and resurrect only when the area is secure.

HP, PvP Damage Tolerance, PvP Accuracy and Status Effect Resist become more valuable because Cleric must survive before it can heal anybody.

The broader Abyss progression is covered in the AION 2 Abyss Guide.

## Best Cleric Stigmas and Upgrade Order

Global has four Stigma slots, opening at character levels 22, 27, 32 and 37. Current Global Stigma progression goes to level 20, so do not copy six-slot or level-25 regional layouts.

Four slots mean every Stigma must justify its place. The best loadout changes with party composition and encounter difficulty.

| Content | Priority Stigmas | When to swap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Boss Healer | Light of Protection; Earth Punishment; Noble Aura; Prayer of Amplification | Default hybrid damage/healing setup. |
| Chanter Party | Absolution; Earth Punishment; Noble Aura; Prayer of Amplification | Remove Light of Protection when Undefeated Mantra already covers the overlapping party buff. |
| New / Difficult Dungeon | Light of Protection; Absolution; Yustiel's Power; Summon Resurrection | Use when survival is more important than personal damage. |
| Solo / AoE | Light of Protection; Earth Punishment; Noble Aura; Prayer of Amplification | Swap utility only if survival or cleansing becomes a real problem. |
| Defensive PvE | Light of Protection; Absolution; Yustiel's Power; Summon Resurrection | Benevolence can replace resurrection in a debuff-heavy dungeon once developed. |
| Arena / PvP | Salvation; Yustiel's Power; Absolution; matchup control | Adjust according to enemy composition and team role. |

### Which Cleric Stigmas Are Worth Investing In?

- Light of Protection: party PvE/PvP Damage Boost and Damage Tolerance. It does not stack with Chanter's Undefeated Mantra; only the stronger effect applies, and the Mantra wins a tie.
- Earth Punishment: the key Condemnation Stigma. At level 5, Condemnation critically hits the affected target. Later milestones add Double Chance, Attack/Defense and longer duration.
- Noble Aura: summons an offensive Aura that attacks automatically. Higher investment increases its damage frequency.
- Prayer of Amplification: an offensive burst Stigma that increases Attack and PvE Damage Boost and should be aligned with Bolt and burst windows.
- Absolution: emergency party recovery and cleansing. Higher investment improves its reliability against multiple debuffs.
- Yustiel's Power: large temporary party PvE Damage Tolerance for predictable heavy damage.
- Summon Resurrection: battle resurrection from long range. Early investment is enough to unlock its tactical value.
- Benevolence: long-duration group healing that becomes especially useful in debuff-heavy encounters at higher investment.

### Recommended Cleric Stigma Upgrade Order

1. Raise the four Stigmas you actually use to their early inexpensive milestones.
2. Take Light of Protection to level 10 for most of its party buff value.
3. Raise Earth Punishment toward level 15 and then level 20 because its uptime directly controls Condemnation reliability.
4. Develop Prayer of Amplification and Noble Aura for the standard damage package.
5. Finish Light of Protection later if you regularly run without a Chanter.
6. Develop Absolution, Yustiel's Power, Summon Resurrection or Benevolence when your regular content requires them.

In a Chanter party, do not waste one of four slots on an overlapping Light of Protection when Undefeated Mantra already provides the stronger or equal effect.

The wider system is covered in the AION 2 Stigma and Daevanion Guide.

## Best Cleric Macros and Manual Skills

Global has an in-game macro system and Skill Queue. Enable Settings → Combat → Controls → Skill Queue. Cleric should automate only repeatable offensive inputs; healing decisions remain manual.

Use the macro as a priority helper, not as a replacement for watching the party and boss.

| Template | Suggested sequence | Keep manual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal Boss / Chanter Party | Earth's Retribution → Judgment Thunder → Lightning Strike Scattershot → Condemnation → Lightning Strike Scattershot → Condemnation → Condemnation | Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, Bolt, Stigmas, Defiance, resurrection |
| New Dungeon | Earth's Retribution → Condemnation → Judgment Thunder → Condemnation | All healing, cleansing and encounter reactions |
| Solo AoE | Earth's Retribution → Judgment Thunder → Condemnation → Condemnation | Pull choice, movement, Bolt charge, recovery |
| Arena / Abyss | No long fixed macro | Healing, cleanse, control, Defiance, Salvation, target decisions |

### Which Skills Should Never Be Fully Automated?

Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, Absolution, Yustiel's Power, Summon Resurrection, Salvation and Defiance should remain on accessible manual controls. Bolt should also be charged manually.

Condemnation appears multiple times in the PvE macro intentionally because a Critical Hit can reset its cooldown and the repeated input catches that reset.

Start with the minimum 10 ms macro delay. If high latency causes dropped inputs, test a larger value such as 40–50 ms instead of assuming one delay works for every connection.

### What Weapon Should Cleric Use?

Cleric uses a Mace and Guard. Early Global progression can use dungeon equipment, while crafted Dragon Lord pieces are attractive long-term because their Soul Binding lines remain valuable through later crafting tiers.

1. Use a functional Mace and Guard from story or early dungeons.
2. Replace pieces with no useful Soul Binding as soon as practical.
3. Use dungeon gear as stepping stones while preparing crafted long-term pieces.
4. Prioritize the Mace first, then Guard and important accessories.
5. Bind expensive lines only on equipment you expect to retain.
6. Keep separate PvP requirements when Arena or Abyss becomes regular content.

### Best Manastones for Cleric

For Mace and Guard, prioritize Weapon Damage Boost, Critical Damage Boost and Damage Boost, then Attack. Armor can use Attack efficiently because Healing Enhancement also converts it into stronger healing.

Front or Back Attack bonuses are conditional. Do not build around directional damage unless your real positioning consistently satisfies the requirement.

Manastones are lost when transferring an item, so do not put your most expensive stones into gear you expect to replace immediately. The AION 2 Gear Progression Guide covers the wider equipment system.

## Best Arcana for Cleric

Arcana should finish useful active and passive skill levels rather than simply maximizing card rarity.

The Global launch build values individual skill lines more than forcing a weak set bonus.

### Which Arcana Bonuses Should You Prioritize?

| Card | God stat | Useful skill lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chalice of Vigor | Time / Combat Speed | Judgment Thunder, Light of Regeneration or any important skill still below target |
| Parchment of Magic | Destiny / MP | Condemnation, Radiant Recovery, Bolt, Chain of Torment |

1. Fill every Arcana slot with useful cards before overspending on one perfect card.
2. Use the Chalice as the flexible card for an important skill that is still below its target.
3. Use Parchment of Magic when MP and its active-skill pool fit the build.
4. Use Compass of Vigor instead if Accuracy is the actual problem.
5. Use an Illusion-oriented option when Cooldown Reduction is more valuable than additional Critical Hit.
6. Compare the finished equipped result before replacing a functioning card.

See the AION 2 Arcana Guide for the wider system.

## Best Pantheon Priorities for Cleric

For live Global PvE, prioritize Destruction for Attack, Wisdom for Double Chance and Illusion for Cooldown Reduction. Time and Freedom are useful secondary directions, while Death gains value when the build still needs Critical Hit.

| Domain direction | Recommended use | What to evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destruction | Primary PvE progression | Attack improves both damage and healing through Healing Enhancement. |
| Wisdom | General offensive progression | Double Chance and useful resource efficiency. |
| Illusion | Cooldown-focused progression | Cooldown Reduction improves Stigma and utility uptime. |
| Time / Freedom | Secondary optimization | Combat Speed or Accuracy according to the actual stat gap. |
| Death | Critical-Hit correction | Useful while Condemnation still needs more permanent Critical Hit outside Earth Punishment coverage. |

Do not delay core gear, Stigmas or important skill thresholds to chase a perfect Pantheon layout.

Collection pieces should reinforce the build you actually play rather than a theoretical endgame setup copied from another region.

## Best Cleric Pets, Genus Insight, and Wings

Pets contribute through collection bonuses and Genus Insight. There is no single premium pet required for Cleric to heal correctly.

### Which Pets Should Cleric Use?

Build the collection around useful permanent stats instead of one supposedly mandatory active companion.

- Attack is useful because it supports damage and Healing Enhancement.
- Accuracy matters when offensive skills or debuffs miss.
- HP and PvE Damage Tolerance improve progression survival.
- Critical Hit remains useful while Earth Punishment has downtime.

1. Develop Condemnation and the core healing skills.
2. Establish the standard four-Stigma Global loadout.
3. Take the important +1 Daevanion active-skill nodes.
4. Reach level 20 on Condemnation, Radiant Recovery and Bolt through Daevanion and rings.
5. Develop Empyrean Lord's Grace, Earth's Grace, Healing Enhancement and Radiant Benediction.

If an upgrade does not improve healing reliability, solve MP problems, improve practical damage or cross a meaningful skill breakpoint, it can usually wait.

## Common Cleric Mistakes to Avoid

- Using pre-launch Launch Scale Test information: Global is already live and the current four-slot systems are known.
- Copying six-Stigma regional builds: Global currently uses four equipped Stigma slots.
- Playing as a passive heal bot: return to damage whenever the party is stable.
- Using Condemnation without Chain of Torment: the damage engine depends on the target condition.
- Ignoring Earth Punishment: its guaranteed Condemnation Critical Hits are central to the live PvE loop.
- Keeping Light of Protection beside an equal or stronger Chanter Mantra: the overlapping effects do not stack.
- Automating emergency abilities: Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, Defiance, resurrection and major defensive Stigmas require player judgment.
- Automatically releasing Bolt: control its charge and use it in the correct window.
- Using every heal immediately: preserve major recovery for dangerous mechanics.
- Ignoring Attack because Cleric is a healer: Healing Enhancement makes Attack part of the healing build.
- Overstacking Critical Hit after Earth Punishment uptime improves: stronger universal multipliers may become better.
- Ignoring MP: long fights can still drain the class despite Earth's Retribution and Defiance recovery.
- Putting expensive Manastones into temporary gear: Manastones are lost on transfer.
- Locking weak Genus rolls too early: raise Insight before expensive rerolling.
- Filling Daevanion evenly: reach the important skill and major-stat breakpoints first.

## AION 2 Cleric FAQ

Can Cleric and Chanter play together in the same party?
Yes. However, Light of Protection and Undefeated Mantra have overlapping party effects and do not stack. Use Absolution or another useful Stigma in the Cleric slot when the Chanter already provides the stronger or equal Mantra.

Does Cleric need a specific premium pet to heal effectively?
No. The healing foundation comes from skills, Attack scaling, passives, gear and progression systems. Pets and Genus provide additional permanent bonuses but no premium companion is mandatory.

Should Cleric carry recovery consumables?
Yes. HP and MP consumables remain useful during emergencies or long fights and should support the skill rotation rather than replace it.

## Conclusion

Live Global Cleric is a hybrid healer that keeps the party alive and immediately returns to damage whenever recovery is not required. Build the offensive core around Chain of Torment, Earth Punishment and Condemnation, support the party with Healing Light, Radiant Recovery and Light of Regeneration, and adapt the four Stigma slots to the actual party. Attack, Soul Binding, Arcana, Daevanion, Pantheon, Genus Insight and Wings should all reinforce the same healer/DPS engine instead of copying later-region setups that do not match the current Global client.
