# AION 2 Patch Notes — August 26, 2026

The largest class patch since Chapter 1, built as one connected argument. A minimum skill-cancel time is imposed on every skill so input speed stops deciding DPS; the Templar's Judgment moves onto shield attacks and Punishment becomes a 20m projectile, while the Chanter's Dark Crush moves onto ranged skills and Spinning Strike is rebuilt to reduce reliance on it; the Gladiator, Templar, Chanter and Cleric lose a large share of the party buffs they were providing, and the lost damage is handed back through a broad per-class DPS raise. Shields across three classes become HP-proportional, core-skill life steal is cut, Abyss set damage amplification doubles at the 8- and 12-piece steps, and AION 2's second Server Transfer window opens alongside the new 'Moonlit Oasis' Daeva Pass. Translated from the official Korean update note.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

## Class Changes

This is the largest class patch since Chapter 1, and it is built as one connected argument. A minimum skill-cancel time is imposed on every skill so that input speed stops deciding DPS; the Templar and Chanter have their core rotations rebuilt off crowd-control triggers; support classes lose a large share of the party buffs they were providing; and the damage that all of that removes is handed back through a broad per-class DPS raise.

### Common

- A minimum skill-cancel time is applied. To reduce the variance in skill chaining, cancel timing and DPS that comes from a player's game environment, a minimum cancel time is applied to all skills.

### Core-skill rework

Given how similar the skill mechanics of the melee classes are, this rework is applied to the Templar and the Chanter first; whether to extend it to other classes will be judged from how it plays out. For the Templar, Judgment's trigger condition moves to 'when using a shield attack skill', and Punishment becomes a ranged skill so that a missed charge costs less. For the Chanter, Dark Crush's trigger moves to 'when using a ranged skill', and Spinning Strike's usability, presentation and impact are improved to reduce the class's dependence on Dark Crush.

### Server Transfer

AION 2's second Server Transfer window, after the first one in the July 22 note. Sold from [In-game Shop] → [Server Transfer], from August 26, 2026 (after regular maintenance) to September 1, 2026 (Tue). Applications can only be made between 20:00 and 24:00 on each day of that window, and each server's inbound and outbound quotas update at 20:00 daily.

| Cost component | Price | Purchase limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base cost | 1,000 Quna | Once per account |
| Per-character cost | 200 Quna per character transferred | — |
| Slot expansion cost | 1,000 Quna per expanded slot | — |

Worked example from the note: transferring 2 characters costs 1,400 Quna, but if every empty slot on the destination server is locked, two slot expansions are added and the total becomes 3,400 Quna.

- Transfers are only possible to a server within the same race.
- Limited to once per account, and only from one server to one server.
- There is no limit on how many characters can move in a single transfer; the cost is calculated from the number selected.
- Each transferring character must be level 45 or above and standing in a town — applications cannot be made from the Abyss, the Spacetime Rift, or a dungeon.
- The character must not be in a Legion, nor have a pending Legion application, and must have no duty quest [Mission] in progress.
- You cannot use the product if you hold unclaimed account or character goods: items listed or awaiting settlement on the brokerage, unclaimed items in the shop storage, or unread mail in a transferring character's mailbox.

The note carries a full item-by-item transfer table of roughly fifty rows. In summary: characters, Quna and mileage, Abyss points, memberships and membership passes, character-level Daeva Passes and Growth Supports, Daevanion, cube inventory and character storage, titles, achievements, closet appearances and collection points, wings, divine stones, Pantheon, Arcana, Monolith, Empyrean Lord's Trace, content reward counts and tickets, season missions and season ranking points, quests, exploration, attendance, skills and Stigmas, crafting, gathering and material-conversion records, friend and block lists, brokerage history, customizing, registered styles and item-recovery history all move with the character and merge with what is on the destination server. Server-level Daeva Passes and Growth Supports cannot transfer if the same one is already active there. Server storage transfers its expansion record only — items left in server storage do NOT move, and neither does Kina held in server storage, so move anything you want to keep into a transferring character's inventory or character storage first. Mail block lists do not transfer. See the official note for the row-by-row table.

Other consequences worth knowing: every transferred character is renamed to '@TempName' even if the name is free on the destination server, and you set a new name on first login there. Duplicate special pets bought from the shop stay on the destination server and you are given a Pet Exchange Ticket instead — or a Daeva Pass Shop Coin: Returning if the pet came from the pass shop — and duplicate un-decoratable cosmetic pieces are later given as a Skin (Single) Exchange Ticket. For Genus Insight you choose between the two servers' progress, and the current server's is reset either way. Character slot expansions already used stay behind on the old server.

Cautions: once an application completes, the transfer cannot be cancelled or reversed; the account is moved to the title screen and cannot log in while the transfer runs; completion is announced by a Purple PUSH notification; a transfer takes at least a minute and may take longer depending on the number of applicants; transfers may be temporarily restricted to keep processing stable; and the product may be sold again in the same or a similar form later.

The Cleric's Earth Punishment is restructured so its buffs give 10% to yourself and 5% to party members instead of 7% to everyone.
- How does AION 2 Server Transfer work in August 2026?This is the second transfer window, running from August 26 to September 1, 2026, with applications only accepted between 20:00 and 24:00 each day. It costs 1,000 Quna as a base fee, once per account, plus 200 Quna per character and 1,000 Quna per slot expansion needed on the destination server. Transfers are same-race only, one server to one server, and each character must be level 45 or above, standing in a town, not in a Legion, and free of in-progress duty quests and unclaimed goods. Items and Kina left in server storage do not move, so anything you want to keep must go into a character's inventory or character storage first.
- What changed for Abyss gear in the AION 2 August 26 update?The Abyss damage-amplification set option is raised at every tier, and the top steps double: the 2-piece bonus goes from 10% to 15%, the 5-piece from 12.5% to 20%, the 8-piece from 15% to 30%, and the 12-piece from 20% to 40%. Abyss boss and Dimensional Core stats are also raised further, and the note says those numbers may be adjusted again.
