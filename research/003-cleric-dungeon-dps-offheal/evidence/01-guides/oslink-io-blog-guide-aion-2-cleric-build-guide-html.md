## AION 2 Cleric Overview

The Cleric is AION 2’s dedicated main healer and support class. It uses a Mace and plays mainly from the back line, keeping the party alive with AoE heals, shields, cleanses, and resurrection skills. This class also has enough offensive power to contribute damage between healing windows, making it very useful in dungeons, raids, and team content. Overall, the Cleric is an S-tier class for group PvE and performs very well in group PvP thanks to its strong healing and unique support abilities.

## Cleric Strengths and Weaknesses

Cleric Strengths:

- The only class with reliable party resurrection.
- Excellent team healing with AoE heals, HoTs, shields, and cleanses.
- Always in demand, making it easier to find groups for endgame content.
- Can deal good damage while also restoring its own mana.
- Has strong defensive skills and good survivability.
- Very useful in group PvP and can also perform well in 1v1 fights.
- Beginner-friendly with a clear role and strong survivability.

Cleric Weaknesses:

- Not the best class for quick single-target burst healing.
- Damage is secondary, so focusing too much on DPS can put the team at risk.
- Often becomes a main target for enemies in PvP.
- Slower solo clear speed compared to pure DPS classes.
- Needs good positioning to get the most value from AoE heals.
- Healing cooldowns and timing are important, especially after the healing changes.

## AION 2 Cleric Core Role and Playstyle

Core Role: The Cleric is the main healer and support for the team. It keeps allies alive with direct healing, shields, buffs, debuff cleanses, and resurrection. When there is a safe opening, Clerics can also deal ranged damage while restoring mana through their attacks.

Playstyle: Clerics mainly stay in the back line and support the team. They use HoTs, AoE heals, and shields before strong enemy attacks to keep the tank and other party members alive. This class needs to stay aware of the battlefield, keep teammates safe, and avoid enemy attacks. Good positioning and timing are also important for Clerics, while dealing damage when there is a safe opportunity.

## AION 2 Cleric Skill Priority

Focus on leveling up these skills to make sure you build your Cleric in the most effective way.

Healing priority:

- Light of Regeneration to keep the group HoT up.
- Healing Light as your main charged heal for stronger group healing.
- Radiant Recovery when several allies need quick healing.
- Absolution and Salvation for heavy damage or emergencies.
- Revive fallen teammates with Summon Resurrection.

Damage / utility priority:

- Keep Debilitating Mark and Chain of Torment active on the boss.
- Condemnation when Chain of Torment is active.
- Cast Divine Aura and Bolt on cooldown.
- Fill gaps with Earth's Retribution (mana restore) + Judgment Thunder.
- Lightning Strike Scattershot when the target is staggered/groggy.
- Prayer of Amplification and Light of Protection for party buffs.
- Defiance for a CC break when needed.

## How to Play AION 2 Cleric

AION 2 Cleric gameplay mainly focuses on staying mobile and keeping the party alive from a safe distance. You have to manage mana, keep important debuffs and buffs active, and deal damage when there is a safe opening. When thinking about the AION 2 Cleric rotation, always prioritize survival and keeping the team alive first, then use your damage and healing abilities at the right time. Below are some key strategies for playing the Cleric:

- Positioning: Stay at a safe range, keep moving, and watch enemy attacks. Stay close to the group when possible for AoE healing.
- Resource Management: Use attacks to restore mana and avoid using charged heals without a good reason.
- Priority Gameplay Order: Keep the party alive → Maintain important debuffs and buffs → Deal damage during safe moments.
- Boss Fights: Use shields before strong attacks, keep steady healing active, and save emergency skills for dangerous moments.
- Macros: Simple attack combinations can help with skill timing, while important charged skills are better used manually.
- Group Play: Communicate with your team about positioning and damage windows. Coordinate defensive buffs with other support classes.
- Solo Play: Use your healing, DoTs, and defensive skills to stay alive while dealing damage.

## Mistakes to Avoid When Building Cleric

When building a Cleric in AION 2, focus on the class's strengths as a group healer instead of only single-target healing. Good timing, positioning, and resource management are important for keeping your party alive.

- Focusing only on single-target healing - Clerics are strongest at group and AoE healing.
- Ignoring Healing Enhancement - This can reduce your overall healing power.
- Using every heal too early - Save strong healing and defensive skills for dangerous moments.
- Confusing HoTs with direct heals - Healing-over-time skills cannot always handle sudden damage.
- Ignoring MP management - Make sure you have enough mana for longer fights.
- Standing in dangerous areas - Keep moving and stay in a good position to avoid attacks.
- Spreading the party too far apart - Stay close enough to make full use of AoE healing.
- Automating important skills - Use resurrection, cleanses, shields, and emergency heals at the right moment.

- Ignoring skill conditions - Learn the conditions of your damage skills to avoid wasting them.
