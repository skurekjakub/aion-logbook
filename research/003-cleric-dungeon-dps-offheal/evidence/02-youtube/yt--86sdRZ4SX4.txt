## Description
Global players just got the Cleric. Korea has had it for ten and a half months - so this guide uses the Korean meta as the map, and sets it up for a global character: four stigma slots and low skill levels.

Sources: 50 official Korean patch notes (Nov 2025 - Oct 2026), about 36 posts on the Inven Cleric (치유성) board and balance board, Korean creators 빵호빵, 두만옥, 강코딩, 치키챠, 리엔TV and 분탕러, and the global client's own skill text.

WHERE IT STANDS
Ninth of nine on the Korean boss boards, at about half the top class's damage; last of eight on global's week-one chart. That's a healer on a damage chart - but Korean parties count the Cleric's damage, and the most-viewed Korean guide found gearing for damage cost no healing.

THE ENGINE
Condemnation was 51% of the damage on a Korean meter. It needs Chain of Torment on the target, and spec 4 resets its cooldown on a critical hit. Earth Punishment makes every Condemnation crit - so every Condemnation resets itself.

THE SETUP
- Left click: Earth's Retribution. Right click: the Macro (Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro). Skill Queue on (Settings → Combat → Controls).
- One column, row 0 first: Earth Punishment → Divine Aura → Condemnation → Chain of Torment
- Macro: one step, that column, 10 ms (17 ms measured slightly better; 50 if your keys lag).
- By hand: Bolt (the Macro fires it uncharged), Healing Light, Radiant Recovery, and your party shield on the pull and before the big hit.

BUILD
Condemnation 20 (specs 2/4/5), Bolt 20 (3/4/5), Divine Aura 20 (3/4/5), Earth's Retribution 16-20, Chain of Torment 10-16, Healing Light 16-20 (1/4), Radiant Recovery 16-20 (2/5).
Passives: Earth's Grace → Empyrean Lord's Grace. Combat speed stops helping at 94.4%; keep cooldown reduction near 33%.

GLOBAL: FOUR STIGMAS
Earth Punishment and Yustiel's Power, then by party: Light of Protection with no Chanter (it doesn't stack with Undefeated Mantra), Prayer of Amplification for damage, or Salvation for raid bosses that wipe parties.
Fresh character: level Condemnation → Bolt and Divine Aura.
Earth's Blessing blocks the Chanter's Power of the Storm - agree who buffs speed.
