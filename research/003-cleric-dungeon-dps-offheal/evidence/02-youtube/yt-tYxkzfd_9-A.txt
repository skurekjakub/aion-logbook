I'm very new but I have some opinions. I'm only at 1200 ilvl so all of this is just off the dummy damage, but I want to share them in case it helps you and others.

1. I really do think having auto-attack on its own key (e.g. LMB) and not part of your macro (e.g. RMB) improves your DPS. I think this game animation cancels and weaves auto-attacks in between your macro executions, but for some reason only if you are also holding down Auto-attack on its own button. My evidence for this that, on the DPS meter, I see both higher DPS if I do this and it is explained by seeing more number of auto-attack casts within the 2min window while still seeing an equivalent number of the big damage casts (Condemnation, Bolt, Divine Aura).

2. I think Bolt at 3 charges is worth it versus Bolt at 1 charge. Whether I take Bolt to 1 or 3 charges, I can fit 7 casts of it in a 2min window, but at 3 charges it does double damage and becomes my #2 highest damage contributor. The terrible part is that, if you make Bolt part of your macro key, it always clips at 1 charge. The best I can do is make Bolt its own key, and hold down 3 buttons. But that really feels shit.

So tl;dr the best setup I have found for me is:

LMB: Earth's Retribution
RMB: Macro
Side-button: Bolt

Macro:
1. Debuff/Condemnation: Debilitating Mark > Chain of Torment > Earth Punishment > Condemnation (10ms)
2. Divine Aura (and Bolt if you are okay with it clipping at 1 charge)

For comparison, DPS comparisons (again I remind you I'm only 1200 gearscore/58k combat power), within a 2min window on the dummy:

1. 3-button setup (Bolt at 3 charges): 9.6k DPS
2. 2-button setup (Bolt clipped at 1 charge): 9.1k dps
3. Your setup (Earth Retribution part of macro): 8.6k dps

I wish I could figure out how do this with just two buttons (without an external macro) because holding down three buttons is really cramp-inducing.

I'll re-test this once I have real gear and have actually completed some proper dungeon content to see if this changes.
