# The Aion 2 look

The web app's visual system: palette, type, layout and the principles behind them. The brief is the spec's § 2 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-aion-logbook-design.md`); the tokens live in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`, and nothing outside that file names a colour.

## Subject, audience, job

- **Subject:** a player's build logbook for Aion 2, the game of winged Daevas.
- **Audience:** the player, between sessions or with the client open on the other screen.
- **Job:** at a glance, which skills to level and to what, which stigma goes in each slot, which macro chain runs on which key.

## Palette

Colour carries meaning, and each meaning comes from what the client uses that colour for.

- **Aether cyan** is interaction and selection: links, the focus ring, the selected thing. The client draws MP and the selected stigma slot in it.
- **Level gold** is progression and the call to act: target levels, priority ranks, the character's level, the roadmap's "now". The client's Level Up and Add Macro buttons are this gold.
- **Glow** is the active tab: the luminous crescent under Mastery and Stigma.

### Contrast

Checked with the WCAG formula over every text-on-surface pair the sheets use. Body and secondary ink clear 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes; the faintest ink (`--ink-3`) clears 4.5:1 on `--surface-2`, the darkest place it sits. Verdict text on its own tint (good, warn, bad), the gold on its tint and the skill-kind badges clear 4.5:1 as well.

## Type

- **Display: Marcellus** (Google Fonts, one weight). A flared roman: stroke ends that swell into the faintest serif, like the client's own titling on the Skill window and its skill names. It sets the `h1`, `h2`, the brand and the character's name, never body text and never figures: its numerals are inscriptional, and "10" reads as "IO".
- **Text: Fira Sans** (400, 500, 600, 700). A humanist sans with an open, slightly calligraphic hand, so it sits beside Marcellus without imitating it, and stays crisp at 11px in chips and tables. Its default figures are lining; tabular figures are switched on wherever numbers line up (levels, ranks, counts, dates). Priority ranks are its bold figures in gold.
- **Code: the system monospace**, for file paths, commands and capture tools only. Numbers and labels are not code, so they stay in the text face.

Scale, on a 14px body, from the classical sequence: 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 24, 36. The page title is 36px on desktop, 28px on a phone. Labels are sentence case; nothing is tracked out in capitals.

## Layout

The shell is kept: a sticky index on the left, the page on the right; on a phone the index becomes a drawer behind a sticky top bar. Content is left-aligned throughout, like the client's own information panel.

Alternatives weighed and dropped:

```
A. a top tab bar across the page            B. the client's skill window, recreated
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐    ┌──────┬────────────────┬──────┐
│ Home  Cleric  Research  Sources  …   │    │ info │   (character)  │ icon │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤    │      │                │ grid │
│ content                              │    │      │                │      │
```

- **A** scatters the class's own pages into a second bar and doesn't survive a growing class list.
- **B** is cosplay: it copies the game's frame instead of answering the job, and its icon grid needs the game's art.

## The memorable thing: the tab glow

The client marks the open tab with a luminous crescent: a bright core that fades to nothing at both ends, with a bloom of light rising onto the label. On the dark theme the core is the brightest point (`--glow-hi`, the sampled highlight); on vellum light can't glow, so the light theme inverts it and the core is the deepest blue. The logbook uses it as its one signature and nowhere else: under the build being shown, and along the index's tree line beside the current page, which lights up where the reader stands. When the reader picks a tab the crescent widens in from its centre; with reduced motion it simply appears.

Everything around it stays quiet: flat panels, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients as decoration.

## Principles

- **Meaning before mood.** A colour appears only for what it means in the client: cyan selects, gold levels, the glow marks where you are.
- **The client's geometry.** Skill badges are squares like the game's icons, chips have 3px corners, panels 6px; only verdict pills are round. No shadows.
- **Wings, drawn here.** The brand mark is an original pair of wings around a diamond, in gold; no game art, no hot-linked icons.
- **Scannable first** (AGENTS.md): badges, colour and position before text; prose clamped.
- **Quality floor:** 16px gutters at phone width, a 2px cyan focus ring on everything focusable, reduced motion respected, both themes at AA.

## Review against the brief and the generic defaults

The first plan, and what changed:

- **Light theme.** First drafted as warm cream (`#f3eee2`) under a high-contrast serif with an orange accent carried over from the existing sheets: the generated-page default. The brief does ask for parchment, so the parchment stays, but it is a greyer, darker vellum (`#eceae3`), the display face is a low-contrast flared roman taken from the client's titling, and the accents are deep aether and bronze, not clay.
- **Dark theme.** A near-black with one bright accent was the obvious move. The sampled slate is bluer and lighter than near-black, and the palette carries two accents with distinct meanings instead of one.
- **Numbers in monospace.** The existing sheets set every number in IBM Plex Mono, a templated tell. Numbers now use the text face's tabular figures; monospace is kept for code.
- **Capitals.** The roadmap's priority badges were tracked-out capitals; they are sentence case now.
- **Middle dots.** The brand line read "Aion 2 · global client"; it is plain words now.
- **Active skills in amber.** The client frames active skill icons in amber (`#f39f45`), but amber sits next to Level gold, and a gold badge would then mean two things. Active skills take the tab-glow periwinkle instead; passive keeps the client's green, stigma the client's magenta "Magic" tag.
- **Ornamental frames everywhere.** Considered thin gold frames on every card; cut, so the tab glow stays the one ornament.
- **Alegreya Sans as the text face.** The first build set the text in Alegreya Sans. Its default figures are oldstyle, so source ids rendered as "oo1", and the `font` shorthand the sheets use everywhere resets both `font-variant-numeric` and `font-feature-settings`, so no global switch could reach every chip. Fira Sans has lining figures by default.
- **Ranks in Marcellus.** Tried in gold Marcellus on the skill priority; its "10" read as "IO" on screen, so ranks moved to bold Fira Sans figures in gold.
- **Cards.** The existing ruled lists (priority, stigma slots, takeaways) are kept as single panels with rules between rows, rather than a card per item.
